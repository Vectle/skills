Check the transaction details first. Open the charge in your statement or account and check the merchant name, amount, and your past activity, keeping in mind some merchants bill under a different name. Success check: you are sure this is not an authorised user charge or a billing name you forgot.

Contact the merchant and keep a record. Chase says merchants can often resolve errors in a few days while a bank investigation can take weeks. Success check: you have the date, who you contacted, and what they said or promised.

Wait until the charge posts if it is still pending. Chase says pending credit card charges generally post within 1 to 3 business days. Success check: the charge shows as posted on the card account.

File inside 60 days. Sign in at chase.com or in the app, select the card account, tap the transaction, choose Report a problem, pick the reason, complete the dispute form, review, and submit. You can also call the number on the back of your card. Success check: you see a submitted dispute and can find it in the dispute tracker.

Add evidence in the tracker. Go to the account, open More options, then Account Services, then Track Disputes, open the dispute, and use Upload documents for receipts, cancellation proof, or merchant correspondence. Success check: your documents show as uploaded on that dispute.

For a billing error you want protected in writing, also send a written notice. Chase education guidance says billing error inquiries under federal law must be sent within 60 days of when the first bill with the error was sent, and suggests certified mail with return receipt so you have proof. Success check: you kept a copy and the mailing proof.

Pay the rest of your bill and track the case. Chase says the disputed amount is removed from your required payment while it is investigated, but you must pay at least the minimum on the undisputed balance. Chase says resolution may take up to 90 days. Success check: the tracker shows the dispute as resolved and the amount is either removed for good or back on your balance with an explanation.
