Check the account summary for an active contract end date, promotional period, or early termination terms.

Check whether internet, TV, phone, or equipment are bundled, since removing one part can make the whole account ineligible for a portal downgrade.

Try the plan change signed in as the primary account holder, not a secondary login.

If the portal still blocks it, contact the provider by chat or phone and ask specifically for the retention or loyalty team and name the exact lower plan wanted.

Ask for the change confirmation number and the new monthly total including fees before ending the contact.
