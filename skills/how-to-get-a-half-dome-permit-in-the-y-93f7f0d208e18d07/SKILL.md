Pick your route first: the preseason lottery in March if you are planning months ahead, the two-day-ahead lottery if your trip is already close. Most people enter both, in that order.

For the preseason lottery, apply on Recreation.gov any time from March 1 to March 31. One application covers up to 6 people and up to 7 date or date-range choices, ranked in your order of preference. Success check: the application appears in your Recreation.gov account.

Name an alternate permit holder during the application. You cannot add one later, and on hike day either the holder or the named alternate must be present with the group.

Pay the 10 dollar non-refundable application fee and watch for results in mid-April. If you win, the 10 dollar per person recreation fee is charged and the permit is issued for your date. About 225 day-hiker permits are available per day.

If you lose, use the daily lottery in season: apply on Recreation.gov two days before your hike date, between midnight and 4 pm Pacific. Results arrive that evening, in time to plan the hike or pivot to another day.

On hike day, bring photo ID and keep the whole group together at the ranger check at the base of Sub Dome. No permit holder or alternate present means no climb, whatever your paperwork says.

## Community-reported tips (not the agency's published rules)

The tips below come from trip reports, hiking forums, and permit guides, not from the agency. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- The park's published lottery statistics, recapped across 2026 permit guides: in 2024 the preseason lottery succeeded on about 22 percent of applications and the daily lottery on about 19 percent, but the odds on any single peak season Saturday run closer to 1 percent per date choice.
- Day of week moves the daily lottery a lot: weekday success runs around 22 percent against about 14 percent on weekends, so a Tuesday to Thursday hike date is worth real percentage points in both lotteries.
- Use all seven preseason date choices, spread across midweek dates in late May, early June, late September, and early October instead of stacked on July Saturdays; guides that model the draw put a flexible application well above 30 percent.
