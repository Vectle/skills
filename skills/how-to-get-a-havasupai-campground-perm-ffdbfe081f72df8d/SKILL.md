Create your account on havasupaireservations.com well before the sale and settle your dates and group: bookings cover a fixed 3 nights and up to 12 people. Success check: you can log in and open the campground booking flow.

Choose your window: paid early access (in 2026, January 21 at 8 am Arizona time through January 31 at 5 pm, for an extra 40 dollars per person, non-refundable) or the general sale on February 1 at 8 am Arizona time.

Be logged in before the sale opens and book the moment it does. Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so 8 am Arizona is 7 am Pacific in winter. Spring and fall dates can sell out within hours of the general sale.

Pay in full at booking: 455 dollars per person for the campground in 2026. Success check: a confirmation in the name that exactly matches your photo ID.

If your dates sell out, keep checking the official site in the following weeks: cancelled reservations return to the site's own inventory. Cancelling 90 or more days before a start date returns 50 percent of the permit fee, and that refund policy is what feeds the returned inventory.

Before the trip, plan check-in: permit holders check in with photo ID at Grand Canyon Caverns Inn the day before or the day of the start date to collect wristbands and tags. Failing to check in cancels the booking with no refund.

## Community-reported tips (not the Havasupai Tribe's published policy)

The tips below come from trip reports and hiking guides, not from the Tribe. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- Checkout runs on a short timer, reported at about two minutes, which is why every current guide insists the account and the saved card be finished before February 1 rather than during the sale. Fumbling a step mid-checkout can cost the dates.
- After opening day, cancellations are the way in, and they tend to surface as trips cross the 90 day refund line, so checking gets more productive about three months before the start dates you want.
- Midweek start dates, and dates in March or late October, survive the opening rush far longer than spring and fall weekends, which can go within minutes.
