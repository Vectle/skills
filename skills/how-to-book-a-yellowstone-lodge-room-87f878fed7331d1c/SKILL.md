Work out your release date: take your arrival month and year, subtract one year, and mark the 5th of that month. Success check: you can name the exact 5th when your whole arrival month opens.

Decide your lodge and room type, plus two backups, before release day, and check the lodges season opening and closing dates since most lodges are seasonal. Success check: you have a first choice and fallbacks that are actually open for your dates.

Book online at midnight Mountain Time on the 5th for high demand lodges and summer dates. Success check: your reservation completes online and you get a confirmation, hours before phone booking even opens at 7 a.m.

If your stay starts in the newly opened month, use the extension rule if you need it: you may add up to 4 continuous nights into the following month, in the same lodge and same room type. Success check: the extra nights appear on the same reservation without waiting for the next release.

If you prefer to book by phone, call the reservations line from 7 a.m. Mountain Time on the 5th, and expect long waits on release days. Success check: an agent confirms the same dates and room type you wanted online.

If your month is already sold out, keep checking the online system for cancellations, and book any night you can get rather than waiting for a perfect block. Success check: cancelled rooms you grab complete checkout and show in your reservations.

Book dinner and activity reservations separately once those schedules open, since they do not follow the lodging window. Success check: lodging, dinner, and activities each have their own confirmation.
