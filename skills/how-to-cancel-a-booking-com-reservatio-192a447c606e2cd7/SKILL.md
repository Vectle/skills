Open your confirmation email or sign in and open the booking, then read the cancellation policy line word for word and note the exact free cancellation deadline. Success check: you have a date, a time, and the fee that applies after it.

Treat that deadline as running in the property local time, not your time, and cancel well before it if you want it free. Success check: your planned cancel time is safely before the deadline in the property time zone.

Check who charges the card. Generally the property charges it, and Booking.com charges it only when your confirmation says payment is handled by Booking.com. Success check: you know which name to expect on your statement and who would process any refund.

Cancel inside the booking using the cancel option and complete every confirmation screen, rather than just closing the page or emailing the property. Success check: the booking status changes to cancelled on screen.

Save the cancellation confirmation email and screenshot the cancelled status. Success check: you hold written proof with the cancellation time on it.

Watch your card for any prepayment refund or the release of a temporary hold, and follow up with whoever charged you if it does not move. Success check: the refund or released hold shows on your statement, or you have opened a follow up with the charging party.
