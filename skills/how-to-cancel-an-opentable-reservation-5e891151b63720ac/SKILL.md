Open your confirmation email or your OpenTable account and find the reservation under upcoming reservations. Success check: you are looking at the right restaurant, date and time.

Check the restaurant cancellation policy shown at booking and in the confirmation email. Some places allow same day cancellation, others need 24 to 48 hours, and some charge for late cancels. Success check: you know your deadline and any fee before you cancel.

Choose Modify if you just need a new time, date or party size, or Cancel if you cannot come at all, then confirm. On the website use your profile or calendar view, in the app use the reservations section. Success check: you reach a confirmation page for the change or cancellation.

Keep the updated confirmation or cancellation email. Success check: your inbox shows the reservation as cancelled or modified, not still upcoming.

If no cancel option appears, contact the restaurant directly before the seating and get the cancellation acknowledged. Success check: the restaurant confirms you are cancelled, so its system does not mark you as a no show.

Never just skip a booking you cannot use. Success check: every unwanted reservation ends as cancelled or modified, not as a no show on your account.
