Check your date is a Tuesday to Saturday dinner date and inside two months from today. Success check: the date is not a Sunday or Monday and not more than two months out.

Open the Providence listing on OpenTable, or call the restaurant, and search your party size and date. Success check: real seating times appear, with the earliest around 5:30 pm on Friday and Saturday and 5:45 pm on other open days.

If your first choice is gone, try the other channel and nearby dates before giving up. Success check: you have checked both OpenTable and a phone call, or a second date.

Complete the booking with the credit card required to hold it and add dietary restrictions in the reservation notes. Success check: you get a confirmation for the right date, time and party size.

Note the cancellation terms in your confirmation. Success check: you know the deadline and the per person late cancel or no show charge that applies to your booking before the day arrives.
