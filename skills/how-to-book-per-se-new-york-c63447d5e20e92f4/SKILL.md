Work out your release day first. For a dinner in a given month, bookings open on the 1st of the prior month on Tock. Success check: you have the 1st marked and you know your first choice date plus one backup date.

Check the current release notice on the Per Se Tock page before that day for the exact opening time for that cycle. Tracker guides report 10 AM Eastern as the recent pattern, but Per Se announces cycles separately and times have varied, so trust the live Tock notice over any guide. Success check: you know the posted time for your cycle, not just the date.

Create or sign in to your Tock account in advance and save a payment card. Success check: you are already signed in before the release, with no account setup left to do.

At the release, search Per Se on Tock, set your party size, and pick the Main Dining Room experience. Current Tock listings reported in November 2026 price the tasting at 425 dollars per person, with a 200 dollar per person deposit applied to the bill. Success check: the experience name, date, and party size match before you pay, and the deposit terms on screen match what you expected.

Take the first acceptable seating instead of holding out for prime time. Early and late seatings are part of the same menu. Success check: you complete checkout and get a Tock confirmation for a real date, rather than watching prime slots vanish.

Add dietary needs and the occasion in the booking notes, then keep the confirmation. Success check: your notes are saved on the reservation and the deposit shows as credited toward the final bill.

If the main room sells out, search again and switch the experience to the Salon. It is listed separately from the main room. Current listings price it as a four course menu at 185 dollars per person, with a 150 dollar per person deposit applied to the bill, for smaller parties. Success check: Tock shows Salon availability even when the main room shows none.

If both are full, keep checking Tock for cancellations and be ready to book quickly when a table returns. Success check: you book the returned table in your own Tock account at the restaurant price.
