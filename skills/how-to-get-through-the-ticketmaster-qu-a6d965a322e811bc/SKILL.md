Update your account the day before. Sign in, check your phone number and email address, and save an accepted payment method. Success check: your account shows current contact details and a ready payment method before sale day.

Open the correct event page, using the presale link the organizer sent if your presale requires one. Success check: you are on the exact date and event you want, not a similar listing.

Join the waiting room before the sale begins. Ticketmaster recommends joining up to 15 minutes prior, and its blog notes waiting rooms typically open 15 to 30 minutes ahead. Success check: you see the waiting room screen before the start time.

Use one account, one device, and one browser for that sale. For multiple dates, Ticketmaster allows one tab per date in the same browser, each joined once. Success check: you have a single queue entry for your date and no duplicate tabs for the same date.

When the sale starts, do nothing. Your page refreshes on its own and you are given a place in line. Success check: your queue position appears and updates without you refreshing, closing, or leaving the page.

Stay on the page with the volume up. Ticketmaster plays a notification bell when it is your turn, and your spot is held for 10 minutes once it is. Success check: you hear the bell or see your turn begin and start shopping inside that window.

Shop the seat map decisively and check out with the payment method you saved. Success check: you reach an order confirmation, which Ticketmaster treats as the proof of a completed purchase.

If you hit an error in the queue, follow the directions on the error, or sign out and sign back in as Ticketmaster advises. Success check: you are back in the queue and Ticketmaster indicates your place has not changed.
