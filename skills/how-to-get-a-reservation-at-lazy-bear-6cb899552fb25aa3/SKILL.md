Mark the 15th of the month before your target month, at 10am Pacific. For example, tickets for November are released on October 15th. Success check: you have the release date and time in your calendar with a reminder before it.

Create or sign in to your Tock account in advance and save your payment details, then open the Lazy Bear page from the restaurant own site ticket link. Success check: you are logged in and can reach checkout without a password reset.

Pick two or three acceptable dates and seating times in advance, in priority order, covering the 5pm through 9pm range. Success check: you have a fallback date ready if your first choice sells out.

At 10am on release day, buy the tickets for your party of 1 to 6 and complete checkout. Expect a 20 percent service charge plus local tax on top of the $320 ticket at checkout. Success check: Tock shows the tickets in your account and you get a confirmation.

Answer the dietary questionnaire right away and flag restrictions at least 48 hours before dinner. Lazy Bear accommodates many restrictions but cannot remove butter, so there is no vegan menu. Success check: your restrictions are recorded on the reservation well before the 48 hour cutoff.

Arrive on time. If you are running more than 10 minutes late, call the restaurant and leave a message, since a late start can mean skipped courses. Success check: you are seated for the full menu at a comfortable pace.
