Sign in to your Ticketmaster account and open My Tickets, then select the event. Success check: you can see the order and the individual tickets you want to sell.

Tap Sell to check eligibility. Success check: the flow lets you select tickets and continue, or it tells you resale is not enabled for this event, in which case you stop here.

Select the exact tickets to sell and continue. Note the split rules for reserved seats: two tickets always sell as a pair, four sell as 2 or 4, and general admission tickets can sell in any quantity. Success check: the selected tickets and sale grouping match what you intend.

Set your price. Compare the other listings in your section shown during setup, and stay inside the allowed range: some organizers set a minimum or maximum, and you may only be able to list above or below face value if the organizer terms and local law allow it. The setup shows your expected payout after the service fee before you confirm. Success check: the price is accepted with no range error and the payout figure is one you accept.

Complete the seller checklist in Seller Details: add a debit card or a bank account as your payout method, add a card as the reimbursement method in case the buyer must be refunded, and submit your tax details. If you pick a bank account, verify the two small deposits Ticketmaster sends, which can take a few business days to appear. Prepaid cards are not accepted for payout. Success check: Seller Details shows a payout method, a reimbursement card, and tax details with no outstanding tasks.

Review the listing and post it. Success check: the tickets show as listed on your Listings page with the right event, seats, and price.

Watch for the sale notice. Ticketmaster emails you if the tickets sell and sends the tickets to the buyer automatically. You can edit the price or remove the listing any time before it sells. Success check: a sold listing shows as sold and your unsold listing, if any, is updated or removed when you decide.

Get paid after the event. With the checklist complete, payout typically arrives within 7 business days after the event. Success check: the deposit lands in your chosen payout method, and if a Payout on Hold message still shows more than 7 days after the event, you contact Fan Support.
