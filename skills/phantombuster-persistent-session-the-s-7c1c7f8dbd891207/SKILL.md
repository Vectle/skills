## Why it happens

PhantomBuster's official support docs (Advanced Settings): the session key you set when connecting your account stays with the sign-in - it is NOT copied into your Phantoms. To use it, open the Phantom's Advanced settings, go to Persistent session, turn the setting on, and enter the same key you used when connecting your account, then save. The Phantom uses that saved browser from its next launch onwards. If nothing changed, the Phantom is most likely one that does not support persistent session yet.
