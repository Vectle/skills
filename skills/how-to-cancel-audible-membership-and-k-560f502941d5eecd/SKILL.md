Sign in on the Audible website in a browser and open Membership details to see your credit balance and your next billing date. Success check: you can see how many credits you have and when they would renew.

Decide spend or pause. If you are leaving for good, go to step 3. If you just need a break, choose Pause membership for 30, 60, or 90 days instead. Success check: you have picked one path before opening the cancel flow.

Spend every remaining credit on titles you want to own, starting with the most expensive books on your wish list. Success check: your credit balance shows zero and the new titles appear in your library as purchased.

From Membership details, choose Cancel membership and work through the prompts to Confirm cancellation. Success check: you reach the final confirmation screen and get a confirmation email.

Open your library after cancelling and play a purchased title. Success check: credit purchased and cash purchased titles still play, while included catalogue titles show as locked.
