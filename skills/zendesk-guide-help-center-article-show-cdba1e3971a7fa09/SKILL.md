## Why it happens
Product changes update billing and marketing pages first, while the Guide article keeps the old numbers because no step in the change checklist owns help center pricing, so customers keep seeing the stale price.

## Edge cases
- The symptom appears in one locale, collection or audience view only. Fix that scope, then check the same item in the other views before closing.
- A cached or indexed copy keeps showing the old state after the fix. Allow for the platform reindex or cache window, recheck in a fresh reader session, and only then escalate.
- Two items have near identical titles. Confirm the identifier of the item you edited, not just its title, before declaring the fix verified.
