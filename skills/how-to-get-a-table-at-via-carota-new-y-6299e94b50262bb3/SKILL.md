Pick your daypart on purpose. The restaurant at 51 Grove Street opens at 11am Monday to Thursday and at 10am on Saturday and Sunday, and serves the full menu from open to close without a pause. Aim for opening, lunch, or mid afternoon for the shortest queue. Success check: you arrive outside prime dinner hours with your complete party or a plan for everyone to be there when called.

Go to the host in person. You cannot join the waitlist remotely, and you can only join it if there is a wait at the time you arrive. Success check: the host either seats you or takes your name for the next available table.

Take the estimated return time seriously. The host gives you a time based on the queue and expects your complete party back at that time. Parties of three or more may leave a phone number and will be contacted when the table is almost ready. Success check: you have a clear return time or a phone contact plan and everyone knows it.

Use the wait well. The bar is offered to guests on the waitlist, and the restaurant points waiting guests to its bar across the street for an aperitivo. Success check: you stay close enough to return on time with your full party.

Be ready to be seated when your turn comes. Seating is for complete parties only, so do not send one person to hold a plan for a group that is still travelling. Success check: the whole party is present and you are seated without losing the table.
