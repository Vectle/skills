Sign in on opentable.com or open the OpenTable app and search the restaurant with your date, party size, and preferred time. Success check: you can see the available times, or confirm that no matching table is available.

If no matching table is available, select Notify me. If you are eligible for priority access the button may say Priority notify instead. Success check: the alert setup shows your restaurant, date, party size, and start and end time fields.

Review or update the party size, date, and time range, confirm your contact details, choose SMS if that flow offers it, then select Set alert. Success check: you see confirmation details for the created alert.

Make sure the right notifications are on for the channel you used, push for an app alert, and keep your phone nearby. Success check: your active alert appears in the app Reservations area under Alerts, or on its confirmation page.

When an alert arrives, tap it right away and complete the booking. Success check: you finish checkout and get a reservation confirmation, not just the alert.

To change or cancel an alert later, open the confirmation page or the active alert in Alerts, then use Modify to update it or Delete to remove it. Success check: you see the updated confirmation or the delete success message.
