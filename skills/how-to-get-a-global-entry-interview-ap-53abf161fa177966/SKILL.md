Create your own Trusted Traveler Programs account, complete the Global Entry application, and pay the 120 dollar non refundable fee. Every applicant, any age, needs a separate account and a separate interview. Success check: your application shows as submitted in your TTP account.

Wait for conditional approval. CBP says 80 percent of applications are approved within 2 weeks, while some take up to 12 months or longer. Success check: your TTP account shows conditional approval and instructs you to schedule an interview.

Try booking an enrollment center slot in your TTP account right away, and search other centers you can realistically reach, sorted by soonest available. Success check: you hold a confirmed appointment at a named center, even if it is months out, as a fallback.

Keep hunting for an earlier slot. Check back often for cancellations, and check on the first Monday of the month by 9 am local time, when CBP has released batches of new slots at many centers. Success check: you either move your appointment earlier or you have checked across a release and several ordinary days.

If you travel internationally before your appointment, use Enrollment on Arrival instead. Land at a participating airport, follow the signs to the CBP officers who handle it, and ask for the interview during your entry inspection. No appointment is needed. Success check: the officer completes the interview during that arrival and your TTP account later shows enrollment complete.

Bring the right documents. For an enrollment center interview, bring a valid passport and one other form of ID such as a driver license, plus your machine readable permanent resident card if you are a lawful permanent resident. For Enrollment on Arrival, bring every passport you travel on and a proof of residency document such as a driver license with a current address, a mortgage statement, a rental statement, or a utility bill. Success check: you arrive with the full document set for the path you chose.

After the interview, watch your TTP account for the final decision and your membership details. Success check: your account shows active membership, which also includes TSA PreCheck benefits.
