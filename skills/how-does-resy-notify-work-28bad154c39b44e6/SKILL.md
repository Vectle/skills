Find the restaurant on resy.com or in the Resy app and search your date. If no times are open, look for the Notify button with the bell icon where booking options would normally appear.

Tap Notify and set your party size, date, and the time range you would accept. Success check: the restaurant and date appear in the Notify list in your Resy account.

Turn on push notifications for the Resy app and use an email you actually read. Alerts go out in real time and a popular table can be gone in seconds.

When an alert arrives, open it immediately and complete the booking like a normal reservation. Success check: a confirmation email arrives. The alert by itself is not a reservation.

If you miss the table, stay on the list. Openings cluster near the restaurant's cancellation deadline, often 24 hours before service, so the same date commonly fires again in the last day or two.

## Community-reported tips (not Resy's published policy)

The tips below come from diner reports and reservation guides, not from Resy. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- An alert is not a place in line. The same opening is pushed to everyone watching that restaurant and date at once, the table is not held for you, and it goes to whoever taps through and finishes booking first.
- Eligible American Express Platinum and Centurion cards carry Priority Notify, which fires ahead of the standard alert at participating restaurants, so a standard alert can start a step behind without you knowing.
- Diners report that standard alerts sometimes arrive batched, minutes after a table has opened and gone. The reliable counter is to check the app yourself right at the restaurant's cancellation deadline, usually 24 or 48 hours before service, when diners dodging the fee release their tables.
