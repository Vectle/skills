Start on the restaurant site and follow its Resy link, or call the number listed there. Success check: you are on the Minetta Tavern Resy page or speaking to the restaurant, not a third party reseller.

Set party size and check dates up to about 30 days out, starting with your first choice and one backup date. Success check: you can see live times for your party size, even if they are early or late.

Take the first acceptable dinner time and finish the booking in one pass. Success check: you get a Resy confirmation for the right date, time, and party size.

If dinner is full, set Resy Notify for a range of times you would actually take and check back for cancellations. Success check: Notify is on and your phone alerts are enabled.

If you try the bar, arrive before the dinner rush, ideally before doors fill, and ask the host about bar seating for your full party. Success check: the host seats you at the bar or gives you a clear wait, with the full menu available at the bar.
