Identify your membership type from your agreement or account before you start: Monthly Payment, or limited term or prepaid. Success check: you can name your type and you know whether the online route applies to you.

Go to the Cancel Membership page on 24hourfitness.com. You will see a retention offer of 30 percent off your next 3 months dues, which the page notes is for Monthly Payment members. Success check: you are on the official cancel page and can see both the Yes and No thanks options.

Monthly Payment members: choose No thanks, the option that says you still want to cancel, and follow the account prompts to confirm. Success check: you reach a cancellation confirmation on screen or by email and you save it.

Limited term or prepaid members: do not hunt for an online cancel button that is not offered. Go to a club and ask a team member to process the cancellation, or use the contact link on the cancel page. Success check: a team member accepts your request or your contact submission is sent, and you ask for written confirmation with the date.

Check what your agreement says about timing and fees, including any commitment terms and the annual fee, before your next billing date. Success check: you know your last day of access and whether any final charge is expected.

Keep access expectations straight. Cancellation normally runs through the period you have already paid for rather than cutting access off the same day. Success check: your confirmation shows an end date you can compare with your billing cycle.

Watch your statements for the next cycle or two after cancelling. Success check: no dues post after your confirmed end date, or you dispute an extra charge promptly with your confirmation in hand.
