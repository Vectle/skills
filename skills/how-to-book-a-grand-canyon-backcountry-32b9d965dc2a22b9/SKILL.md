Work out your start month and find its lottery window in the NPS schedule. For a May start, the window opens December 16 and closes January 1 at 5:00 pm MST. Success check: you can name your exact open and close dates from the NPS lottery schedule table.

Create a Recreation.gov account and, during the window, open the Grand Canyon backcountry permit page, find the Early Access Lottery section, and submit an application. The lottery entry itself does not ask for your itinerary. Success check: you have paid the 10 dollar lottery charge and have an application on file before the 5:00 pm MST close.

Check your result on the 2nd. Up to 750 applicants are awarded a date and time slot, with the first slots starting at 8:00 am MST on the 4th. Success check: you either hold a specific time slot or know you are waiting for general access.

At your time slot, log in, open the Early Access Period section, view availability, and book one permit with a named campground or use area for every night, then pay. Success check: you hold a permit that lists the trip leader, party size, dates, and each night's camp, at 10 dollars per permit plus 15 dollars per person per night below the rim or 4 dollars above the rim.

If you did not win a slot, use the general access opening for lottery applicants later that month, and then the public opening at 8:00 am MST on the 1st of the following month, to book any remaining space. Success check: you have checked both fallback openings for your month.

For trips inside three months, skip the lottery and check the Available Permits section on Recreation.gov directly for open space and cancellations. Success check: you can see live availability for your dates without a lottery entry.

Print your permit, carry it on the hike, and attach it to your pack or tent in plain view once camp is set. Success check: your printed permit matches your itinerary, dates, and group size exactly.
