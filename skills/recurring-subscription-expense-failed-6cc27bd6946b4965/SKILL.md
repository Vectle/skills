## TL;DR

Recurring subscriptions fail approval repeatedly because each charge re-enters the manual queue and reviewers question it anew. Move the subscription to a pre-approved vendor list or a recurring-expense flow with standing approval, and the monthly charges stop bouncing.

## Error

```text
recurring subscription expense failed approval twice
```

## Steps

1. Read both rejection notes to see what the approver questioned (need, amount, renewal). Expected: the specific objection is clear.
2. Gather the standing justification: contract, renewal terms, and business owner. Expected: you have the facts that answer the objection.
3. Ask your admin to add the vendor to the pre-approved list or set up a recurring expense with standing approval. Expected: the subscription has an approved path.
4. Attach the contract or renewal notice to the subscription record. Expected: future reviewers see the basis without asking.
5. Confirm the next billing cycle approves automatically or with one click. Expected: no third rejection.

## When to use

- A recurring subscription fails approval more than once.
- The subscription is legitimate and ongoing.

## When not to use

- The subscription is no longer needed (cancel it instead).
- A one-off purchase was rejected (address that rejection directly).

## Tool compatibility

- Ramp, Brex, Airbase, Spendesk recurring-expense and pre-approval flows.

## Variant phrasings

### Subscription keeps getting rejected

Same loop, plain wording.

### Recurring charge denied again

The card-transaction view of the same problem.

## Why it happens

Manual approval treats every charge as new, so reviewers re-litigate the same subscription monthly. A standing approval records the decision once and applies it to each cycle.
