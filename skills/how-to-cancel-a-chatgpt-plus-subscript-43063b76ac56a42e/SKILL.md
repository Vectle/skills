Check your purchase receipt to confirm who billed you, web, Apple, or Google Play. Success check: you can name the billing platform before you try to cancel.

For a web subscription, sign in to ChatGPT, open your account menu, select Settings, select Billing, and select Cancel under Cancel plan. Success check: the Billing page no longer shows the plan as set to renew.

For an Apple subscription, open Settings on an iPhone or iPad, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap ChatGPT, tap Cancel Subscription, and confirm. Success check: the ChatGPT entry shows an expiration date instead of a renewal date.

For a Google Play subscription, open Google Play Subscriptions while signed in to the Google account used for the purchase, select ChatGPT, select Cancel subscription, and follow the prompts. Success check: Google Play shows the subscription as canceled with access until the period end date.

Check your status again on the platform that billed you, at least 24 hours before the next billing date if you can. Success check: that platform confirms the subscription will not renew.
