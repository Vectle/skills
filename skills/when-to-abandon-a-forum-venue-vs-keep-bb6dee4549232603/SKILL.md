## TL;DR

Drop a forum when the pattern is clear: repeated flags or bans, zero traffic after a month of real effort, dead moderation queues, or a community that plainly doesnt want what you offer. Keep trying when the friction is early-stage and normal, like new-account limits or one false-positive flag. The decision rule is simple: two strikes on moderation, or thirty days with no return, and the venue goes on the dropped list.

```text
when to abandon a forum venue vs keep trying
```

## Use this when

- A forum keeps flagging or restricting your account
- You have posted for weeks with no traffic or engagement
- You need a clear rule for pruning your venue list

## Not for this skill when

- You are dealing with a single incident (use the appeal or unflag skills)
- The venue is brand new and still in warming
- You want to evade a ban rather than decide

## Steps

1. Score the friction. Count moderation incidents in the last 30 days: flags, held posts, warnings. Expected: a number, not a feeling.
2. Score the return. Check referral traffic and replies to your posts over the same period. Expected: visits and engagement per hour invested.
3. Apply the two-strike rule. Two moderation incidents in 30 days means the venue's filters dislike your pattern; fix the pattern or drop the venue. Expected: a decision trigger.
4. Apply the thirty-day rule. Real effort for a month with zero measurable return means the audience isnt there or isnt interested. Expected: a second decision trigger.
5. Check for fixable causes. Dead mod queue, wrong category, overly promotional tone: if the cause is fixable in one change, try once more. Expected: you dont drop venues over solvable problems.
6. Drop cleanly and record why. Mark the venue dropped with the reason and date; dont rage-post on the way out. Expected: a venue list that only contains live opportunities.

## Variant phrasings

### should I give up on this forum

Two moderation strikes in a month or thirty days with no return: yes, drop it.

### forum venue not working out

Distinguish fixable causes (wrong category, tone) from structural ones (dead forum, hostile mods) before deciding.

### when is a forum not worth the effort

When the hourly return stays at zero past the first month, or the moderation cost exceeds the value.

## Why it happens

Sunk cost keeps agents posting on dead or hostile venues for months: the account is warmed, the tracker has rows, quitting feels like waste. But effort is the scarce resource, and every hour on a dead venue is an hour not spent on a live one.

## Edge cases

- Seasonal forums look dead off-season; check a full year before applying the thirty-day rule.
- A venue that bans AI content outright is a structural no, not a strike; drop it on discovery.
- Sometimes the venue is fine and your content is the problem; get a second opinion on your recent posts before blaming the forum.
- Keep dropped venues on the list with reasons; a forum that was dead last year can revive under new management.
