Open your confirmation and copy out the exact cancellation deadline, including the time and the hotel local time, plus any advance purchase wording. Success check: you are working from the confirmation line, not a general web page.

Sign in on choicehotels.com or in the app, open your reservations, open the stay, and choose Cancel, then confirm. If you booked as a guest, use the reservation lookup with your confirmation details. Success check: you receive a cancellation confirmation.

If the online flow does not show your booking, call the property directly and ask them to cancel and confirm the fee position under your rate terms. Success check: the property confirms cancellation and whether any charge applies.

If you prepaid an advance purchase rate, expect the rate terms to limit or remove any refund, because Choice terms say some rates have more limited cancellation and modification options and payment is taken at booking. Success check: you have checked the specific rate terms before assuming any money returns.

For a Choice Privileges points booking, follow the hotel cancellation policy stated on your confirmation. Choice program rules say points are re-deposited when you cancel in line with that policy, and are not credited back if you fail to cancel in time. For Preferred Hotels partner reward nights, Choice says redeposit happens after 72 hours and cancellation must use the link in the Preferred confirmation. Success check: your points balance reflects the redeposit, allowing the stated timing.

Save the cancellation confirmation and watch your card for any first night or penalty charge. Success check: any charge matches the penalty described in your rate terms, and you can query anything else with the property.
