Sign in or create a StubHub account, then start the listing from the website or mobile app. Success check: you are signed in and on the listing flow for the right event.

Add the ticket details. StubHub asks for the number of tickets you are selling, the seats, and the delivery details in the listing. Success check: the count, section, row, and seats match the tickets in your account exactly.

Set your price using the payout view, not just the headline price. StubHub says you see the full price breakdown, including exactly how much you receive, before you create the listing, and that it charges a 10 percent fee if the tickets sell. Success check: the amount shown as your payout is an amount you accept.

Create the listing and note the delivery type StubHub shows for your tickets. StubHub lists mobile transfer, Flash Seats, barcode, paper tickets, PDF tickets, and pickup tickets for its Last Minute Services locations. Success check: you know which of these your sale will use before a buyer appears.

Upload or prepare delivery early if StubHub offers it. StubHub says it gives delivery instructions based on your ticket type and that you may be able to upload early after creating the listing, while the tickets remain yours until they sell. Success check: your tickets are ready to send the moment a sale lands.

Watch the listing and adjust the price if the market moves. StubHub says you can edit the listing and change the price any time before the tickets sell, from My Tickets and then Listings. Success check: your price still sits sensibly against similar seats as the event gets closer.

Deliver as instructed when the tickets sell, then wait for payout. StubHub says payment is processed immediately after the event and will appear in your preferred payout method, such as your bank account or PayPal, within 5 to 8 business days. Success check: delivery is confirmed in your account and the payout window is counted from the event date, not the sale date.
