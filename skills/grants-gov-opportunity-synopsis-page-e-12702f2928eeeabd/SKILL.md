Copy the opportunity number and the funding opportunity title from the synopsis exactly as shown.

Start a fresh search by that opportunity number instead of refreshing the open page, and open the synopsis from the new result.

Confirm you are signed in and that the workspace or apply entry point is available on the fresh page before starting the download.

Start the application package download and watch the browser download list rather than the page spinner.

Open the downloaded file and confirm the forms list and the opportunity number inside match the synopsis you started from.

If the download still fails on the fresh page, note the exact time and the opportunity number and try once more after signing out and back in.
