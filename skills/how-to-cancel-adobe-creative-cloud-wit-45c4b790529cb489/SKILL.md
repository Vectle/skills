Sign in to your Adobe account and open Plans to find the exact plan type and renewal date on the plan card. Success check: you can name whether the plan is annual paid monthly, annual prepaid, or month to month, and you know the term end date.

Do the math from Adobe formula. Multiply your monthly price by the months left, then halve it. Success check: you have a rough fee figure to compare with what Adobe shows later.

If you are within 14 days of the initial purchase, cancel now through Manage plan and Cancel your plan. Success check: the review screen shows a full refund and no cancellation fee.

If you are near the end of the term, wait until the final billing period, turn off renewal if your plan page offers it, and cancel so the plan ends at the term end with little or no remaining balance. Success check: the review screen shows zero or near zero fee, because there is almost no remaining balance to halve.

If you must leave mid term, start Manage plan and Cancel your plan, work through the reason and offer screens without accepting a new plan unless you want it, and stop at the review screen. Success check: you can see the exact fee in dollars before any charge is final.

Confirm only if the fee on screen matches your math or is zero, then save the confirmation message and check that the plan shows as cancelled. Success check: the plan no longer shows as active and you have the confirmation for your records.

Move any files you need out of cloud storage promptly. Adobe says a cancelled account converts to a free membership with a 5GB limit and you have 30 days to move files if you are over that limit. Success check: your important files are stored somewhere you control.
