Find the confirmation email or text from the venue and open the Manage Reservation or Cancel Reservation link for that booking. Success check: you land on a page showing your exact date, time, and party size.

Read the cancellation policy on that page before you touch anything and note the deadline and the per person fee for late cancel or no show. Success check: you can state the deadline hour and fee amount from your own booking, not from a general guide.

If you are before the deadline, follow the cancel prompts and confirm the cancellation. Success check: the page shows the reservation as cancelled.

Save the cancellation confirmation and check for a follow up email from the venue. Success check: you have written proof with the venue name and reservation date.

If the link is missing, expired, or says the booking cannot be changed online, contact the venue directly by phone and ask them to cancel it and confirm in writing. Success check: the venue confirms the cancellation and whether any fee applies.
