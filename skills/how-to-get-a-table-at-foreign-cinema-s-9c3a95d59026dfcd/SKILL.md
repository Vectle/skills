Work out your date 60 days ahead and set a reminder for 12:00 pm Pacific on the day that date opens. Success check: you know the exact release day and time for your target date.

Open the reservation link on foreigncinema.com, which takes you to OpenTable, and pick the indoor dining room or the partially covered outdoor heated courtyard. Success check: you are on the OpenTable booking page for Foreign Cinema in San Francisco.

Book at noon Pacific sharp for parties up to 10, and include every child in the count. Success check: you hold one confirmed reservation, since duplicate bookings are not honored.

Add a courtyard request to the reservation if the film matters, knowing the film starts at dusk in the courtyard only and the audio is background ambiance. Success check: your request is noted on the booking and you understand it is not a guarantee.

If your date is full, check the restaurant's own sold out list on its homepage and keep checking OpenTable as the date gets closer. Success check: you either find a released table or switch to the walk-in plan below.

For a walk-in attempt, arrive early in the evening, ideally on a weeknight, and ask about both the dining room and the courtyard. Success check: the host can quote you a wait or seat you without a reservation.
