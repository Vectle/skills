## TL;DR
Illegible in a dispute decision means the bank reviewer could not read the document, usually a phone photo of a screen, a compressed screenshot, or a scan under readable resolution. Replace it with the original source: the invoice PDF from your billing system, the delivery record export, the signed document at native resolution. Check every page at 100 percent zoom exactly as a stranger would, keep files inside Stripe size and format limits without crushing quality, and resubmit with fewer, clearer documents rather than a larger blurry bundle.

```text
stripe representment evidence rejected as illegible: how to fix
```

## Use this when
- A dispute was lost or evidence flagged with an illegible or unreadable reason.
- Evidence was assembled from screenshots and phone photos.
- A multi page PDF was compressed hard to fit an upload limit.

## Not for this skill when
- Evidence was read and judged insufficient or irrelevant. Better content, not better resolution, fixes that.
- The dispute deadline already passed with no submission possible. That is a deadline process problem.

## Steps
1. Identify exactly which documents the reviewer flagged, from the dispute outcome details or your processor contact. Verify: the flagged documents are named, not guessed.
2. Regenerate each one from its source system as a native PDF or export and discard photos and screenshots where a source file exists. Verify: each replacement opens as a crisp native document.
3. Inspect every page at full size for readable text, totals, dates, and signatures, and split oversized bundles into clearly named separate files. Verify: a reviewer unfamiliar with the order can read every key field unaided.
4. Upload within Stripe file requirements, confirming format and size per file, and use the evidence fields that match each document type. Verify: the submission shows all files accepted with no warnings.
5. Add a readability checklist to your dispute workflow so future packets pass the full size read test before submission. Verify: the checklist is part of the representment runbook.

## Why it happens
Evidence passes through several lossy hands: staff screenshot a dashboard, message it to themselves, compress it into a PDF, and upload it. Each hop strips resolution. Bank reviewers spend seconds per document and will not zoom and squint; unreadable reads as unproven, and unproven disputes are lost disputes.

## Edge cases / pitfalls
- More pages is not better evidence. One clear invoice beats ten blurry attachments.
- Redactions done with translucent markers become readable and leak data. Redact with solid opaque blocks or do not include the field.
- Keep the pristine originals in the case file. If the bank asks again, resending a recompressed copy restarts the quality loss.
