Open your reservation confirmation and read its cancellation policy first, word for word. Success check: you can state your venue deadline and whether any refund is offered.

If online cancellation is offered, cancel from the reservation in your Tock profile or from the confirmation link. Success check: the reservation shows as cancelled and you get a cancellation notice.

Check the refund outcome against the policy you read. Success check: any refund amount matches the venue policy and goes to the original payment method.

If there is no online cancel option, contact the business directly using the contact details in your confirmation. Success check: you have a reply from the venue, not just Tock, about cancel and refund.

If the venue policy is all sales final, use Transfer from your reservation if it is enabled, instead of cancelling for no refund. Success check: the transfer is completed inside Tock to the new guest.
