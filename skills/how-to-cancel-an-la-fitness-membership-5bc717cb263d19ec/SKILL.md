Check your agreement for your billing date, any initial term, and whether you prepaid last month dues at enrollment. Success check: you can name your next billing date and your term status before you start.

Try your online account first. Sign in on lafitness.com, open your account area, and complete the cancellation flow if it is offered for your membership. Success check: you reach a confirmation screen or message and save a screenshot with the date visible.

If online does not work for you, go in person to a club and ask at the front desk to cancel. Success check: the desk processes the request and you leave with the date and the name of the person who helped you noted down.

Or cancel by mail. Write a short signed notice with your full name, membership details, home club, the date, and a clear statement that you are cancelling, and mail it to the P.O. Box address LA Fitness lists on its Membership Questions page, using registered or certified mail as LA Fitness recommends. Success check: you hold a mailing receipt showing the postmark date.

Time the mail route against the 5 business day rule. Postmark at least 5 business days before your next billing date to avoid another billing. Success check: your postmark date is at least 5 business days ahead of the billing date on your agreement.

Work out your end date if you prepaid last month dues. LA Fitness says those prepaid dues apply to the month after the month bought by your final recurring payment before it received your notice, and the membership expires at the end of that prepaid month. Success check: you can state the exact month your access ends and you keep using the club until then.

Watch the next billing cycle. If your notice was postmarked inside the 5 business day window and you are billed once more, LA Fitness says it will refund that additional billing, so contact the club or member services with your proof. Success check: no further billings appear after that one, or the extra billing is refunded.
