# how to get a reservation at The French Laundry

## TL;DR

All regular tables are sold on Tock, all at once: the full following month opens on the 1st of the month at 10:00 AM Pacific. Seats are prepaid in full like event tickets and are gone within minutes, so the whole game is being logged in, payment ready, and flexible on dates the moment the release opens. If you miss the drop, the Notify list in Tock for your date is the realistic second chance.

## Steps

1. Create a Tock account and save your payment details before release day. Success check: you can reach checkout on any test booking without typing a card number.
2. Work out your release moment: tables for a given month open at 10:00 AM Pacific on the 1st of the month before it. Success check: the exact release date and time are in your calendar, with a reminder 15 minutes early.
3. Line up two or three acceptable dates, ranked, and get your party committed to the fallbacks in advance. Success check: you can say yes to a fallback date without a group text thread.
4. At 10:00 AM Pacific on release day, open The French Laundry page on Tock and book the best available slot immediately, paying in full to confirm. Success check: you hold a Tock confirmation; an unpaid cart is not a reservation.
5. If your date is gone, join the Notify list for that date in Tock and keep checking back, especially in the final two weeks when plans change. Success check: Notify is set for every date you would actually take.
6. For a larger group or a private event, contact the restaurant directly instead of fighting the public drop; private events are arranged off Tock. Success check: you have a reply from the events team rather than a waitlist spot.

## When to use

You have a fixed celebration or a trip anchored on this meal, and you can plan a month or more ahead.

## When not to use

You need a table this week (the public drop cannot help; work the Notify list instead), or your party cannot commit, because the prepayment is non-refundable.

## Edge cases

- Prepayment: the meal is charged in full at booking, currently 425 dollars per person for the standard tasting menus with the service charge included. Treat it like a concert ticket.
- Changes: reservations are non-refundable, but a booking can be transferred to another guest inside Tock, which is the safe way to hand a table to friends.
- Release time display: Tock may show the release in your local time zone; anchor on 10:00 AM Pacific so you do not show up an hour off.
- Special menus and holiday seatings can price higher and vanish first; decide your ceiling before the drop, not during it.

## Community-reported tips (not the restaurant's published policy)

Diners, reviewers and booking trackers report the following. None of it is published by the restaurant, and any of it can change without notice.

1. Tables for two are the hardest seats in the room and usually vanish first. If you can put together a party of four or more, your odds improve a lot, and groups of six are reported to still show availability when every two top is gone.
2. February through early May is the easiest stretch of the year, when Napa tourism slows down. Summer and the October to November truffle season are the hardest times to land a table.
3. Lunch, served Friday through Sunday, is noticeably less competitive than dinner, for the same kitchen and the same menu format.
4. A Notify alert goes to everyone waiting on that date at once, and the first person to claim the table gets it. Keep Tock notifications turned on and act the moment one lands, and check the booking page in the morning, when overnight cancellations tend to surface.
