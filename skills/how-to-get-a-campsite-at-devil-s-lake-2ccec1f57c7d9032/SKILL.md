Pick your campground from DNR descriptions. Quartzite, sites 1 to 100, is mostly open and grassy with 75 electric sites and room for large rigs. Northern Lights, sites 104 to 246, has 71 electric sites and is the only campground with wooded electric sites. Ice Age is non electric only and is farthest from the lake at about one mile. Success check: your first choice matches your power, shade, and rig size needs.

Work back 11 months from your arrival date and put that morning in your calendar. Success check: you know the exact date your window opens.

Create your reservation account and save your party, vehicle, and payment details before that morning. Success check: you can search and check out without stopping to register.

On opening morning, search Devil's Lake for your dates, filter for electric if you need it, and book the first site that fits rather than hunting for a perfect site number. Success check: you get a confirmation for specific dates and a site.

If your first choice campground is full, take the next best fit in the park in this order for most people: a wooded electric site in Northern Lights, any electric site in Quartzite or Northern Lights, then a non electric site in Ice Age. Success check: you hold a real reservation inside the park instead of no reservation.

Keep the confirmation and remember you still need a Wisconsin state park vehicle admission sticker for your vehicle. Success check: you have both the campsite confirmation and a plan for the vehicle sticker before arrival.

If you missed the window, check back for cancellations, especially as the trip gets closer, and use any availability alert the reservation site offers for your dates. Success check: you are watching the same official inventory where cancelled sites return.
