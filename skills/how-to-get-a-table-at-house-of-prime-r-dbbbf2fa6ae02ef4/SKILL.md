Start on the restaurant own site and follow its reservation link to OpenTable, or call 415-885-4605 or 415-885-4606 for reservations. Success check: you are booking through a channel the restaurant itself lists, not a lookalike site.

Search the widest date range you can accept, starting with your target date and then nearby weeknights and late seatings. Success check: you have checked more than one date and time before concluding it is full.

Book the best table you find even if it is months out, since the restaurant publishes no release schedule you can time instead. Success check: you hold a confirmed reservation with a date and time in writing.

For a large party, call rather than booking online, since the restaurant directs large parties to phone for details. Success check: the restaurant confirms your full party size on one booking.

Set an OpenTable alert or recheck the calendar, and call to ask about day of cancellations if your date is close and still full. Success check: you have an active alert and a planned call on the day.

If you go without a reservation, arrive before doors open, 5pm on weekdays or 4pm on weekends, put your name down at once, and plan for a wait measured in hours. Success check: your name is on the list before opening and you have a nearby place to wait.
