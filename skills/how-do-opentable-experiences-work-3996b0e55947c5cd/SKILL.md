Sign in to OpenTable and search by restaurant, date, time, and party size, then look for sections or badges labelled Experiences in the results or on the restaurants page, or browse the Experiences section on the restaurant profile. Success check: you are looking at an Experience listing, not a standard reservation time.

Open the Experience and read the full detail before choosing anything: what is included, the per person price or minimum, dates and times offered, and any age, seating, or duration notes. Success check: you can say exactly what the price covers and what it does not.

Read the cancellation policy and any non refundable terms on the listing and again at checkout. Success check: you know the cutoff for a refund or change for this specific Experience, since terms differ from one Experience to the next.

Choose your date, time, and party size from the options that Experience offers, which may be narrower than the restaurants normal hours. Success check: the time you picked belongs to the Experience, with its menu or event attached.

If the Experience is prepaid, enter payment and review the total, including taxes and fees, before confirming. If it is not prepaid, confirm what you will owe at the restaurant. Success check: the checkout total matches the listing price and inclusions you read.

Confirm the booking and save the confirmation. Success check: the reservation appears under Upcoming or My Reservations and the confirmation shows it as an Experience, with its inclusions and cancellation rules.

To change or cancel, open the reservation and use Modify or Cancel if those options appear. If they do not, contact the restaurant directly using the details in your confirmation. Success check: the restaurant or your account confirms the change or cancellation and what, if anything, is refunded.
