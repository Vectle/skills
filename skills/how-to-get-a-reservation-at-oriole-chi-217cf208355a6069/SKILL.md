Pick your night and count back 90 calendar days. That is the day the date comes into the booking window Oriole describes on its FAQ. Success check: you have the target date and its 90 day count back date in your calendar.

Set up Tock before that day. Sign in, save your payment details, and decide in advance whether you want the dining room, a kitchen table, or the Loft package if it is offered, and which times you would accept. Success check: you can open the Oriole Tock page signed in with nothing left to type at checkout.

Check Tock on the day your date enters the window, and again later that day if it looks full at first. Oriole does not publish a release hour on its FAQ, so do not rely on a single early check. Success check: you have seen the new date on the calendar and either booked it or joined the wait list.

Book the first acceptable seating and prepay. Tock listings for Oriole are prepaid experiences, and current listings checked in November 2026 show different per person prices for the dining room, kitchen table, and winter dates, all including the service charge stated on the listing. Success check: you have a Tock confirmation with the date, time, party size, experience, and prepaid amount matching the listing you chose.

Read the terms on your own checkout before paying. Prices change by date and seating, and the cancellation and transfer wording on that checkout governs your booking. Success check: you know whether your booking can be transferred, rescheduled, or refunded before the money leaves your card.

Tell Oriole about dietary needs in time. Oriole asks guests to alert it at least 24 hours in advance, and to contact it before the reservation to check that a restriction can be accommodated at all. Success check: every restriction has been raised and answered before the 24 hour mark.

If your date is sold out, join the wait list and keep checking Tock. Oriole says if a reservation is not available on the date you want, it is happy to reach out should one come available. Success check: you are on the wait list for that date and still checking other nights in the window.
