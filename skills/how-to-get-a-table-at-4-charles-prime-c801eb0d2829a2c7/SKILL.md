Count back 21 days from the date you want and set a reminder for 9 AM EST that morning. Success check: you know the exact release morning for your target date and you are not checking weeks early or a day late.

Before release morning, sign in to Resy, save your payment details, and decide your party size and your first and second choice times. The restaurant accepts online reservations for parties of up to 6. Success check: you can get to the 4 Charles Prime Rib page and start booking without signing in or hunting for a card.

A few minutes before 9 AM EST, open the restaurant page on Resy with your date and party size set. Refresh right at 9 AM when the new date becomes available. Success check: the new date is selectable instead of greyed out or missing.

Take the first time that works for your group and finish the booking straight away. Do not hold out only for a prime time if an earlier or later time is there. Success check: you get a Resy confirmation for 4 Charles Prime Rib and you can see the $5 per person reservation fee noted for the online booking, with holiday fees possibly varying.

If nothing shows, sign up for the notify list for that date and your preferred window of time, as the restaurant itself suggests. Set alerts for more than one date or time window if your plans allow it. Success check: the notify request is saved for 4 Charles Prime Rib and Resy notifications are turned on so an alert will reach you quickly.

If a notify alert arrives, open it right away and complete the booking in that session. Do not wait to check with the whole group if you already agreed on flexible times in step 2. Success check: the alert turns into a confirmed reservation, not just a table you saw and lost.

Keep checking the release path for a second date in parallel if your first date is flexible. A different night 21 days out gives you a fresh 9 AM chance instead of only chasing cancellations on one sold out night. Success check: you have a live plan for at least two dates, or a confirmed table on one of them.
