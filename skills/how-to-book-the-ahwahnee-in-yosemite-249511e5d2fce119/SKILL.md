Work out your arrival date and count back 366 days. Success check: you know the first day your date can be booked.

Go to the TravelYosemite booking site for Yosemite Hospitality and search for The Ahwahnee for your dates and party. Success check: you see room types for The Ahwahnee itself, not another Valley lodge.

Pick a room type that fits, knowing standard rooms face the rear with a limited view and prime view rooms face Half Dome, Glacier Point, or Upper Yosemite Falls. Success check: the room description matches the view and bed setup you want.

Pay the deposit, which is 100 percent of the first night by card at booking. Success check: you get a confirmation and the deposit amount matches the first night rate.

Mark the refund deadline, 7 days before arrival, in your calendar. Success check: you know the last date you can cancel or change and still get the deposit back.

If nothing is available, check the booking site every morning for cancellations and repeat for each date you could accept. Success check: you are checking at the time of day the Park Service says cancellations become available, in the morning.

Book dinner separately if you want the dining room, because a room booking does not reserve a dinner table. Success check: you have a separate dining reservation if you plan to eat there.
