Identify the biller from your statement or purchase history: LinkedIn, Apple or Google Play. Success check: you know which of the three billed you before you open any settings.

If LinkedIn billed you, go to desktop, choose Me, then Premium features, then Manage subscription, and follow the cancel prompts in the account pages. Success check: you reach a cancel confirmation for the active plan.

If Apple billed you, cancel in Apple subscription settings on your device, or start from the LinkedIn app on an Apple device and follow the handoff to Apple. Success check: the LinkedIn subscription in Apple settings shows as cancelled with an end date.

If Google Play billed you, cancel on LinkedIn desktop, in the app, or in Google Play. Success check: the subscription shows as cancelled in the place you used.

Confirm the end date and that you cancelled at least one day before the next billing date. Success check: Premium shows an expiry at the end of the current cycle and no next charge is scheduled.
