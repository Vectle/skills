Work out which Sunday releases your target week. Releases happen every Sunday at 10:00 am for the corresponding week one month ahead. Success check: you have the release Sunday and time in your calendar with a reminder before 10:00 am.

Set up your Tock account in advance, save your payment method, and decide your party size and your first and second choice dates and times. Success check: you can complete checkout without entering card details from scratch.

Open the n/naka page on Tock a few minutes before 10:00 am and refresh right at the release. Success check: the new week dates appear as bookable.

Pick the first acceptable seating among 5:15pm, 5:30pm, 8:45pm, and 9:00pm and complete the deposit payment without pausing to debate dates. Success check: you get a Tock confirmation for a specific date, time, and party size.

Note any dietary limits before you book. The restaurant says it cannot accommodate no seafood, no soy, no miso, no cooked alcohol, or vegan requests, and other allergies go in the pre visit questionnaire. Success check: everyone in your party can eat the menu as described.

Confirm the reservation by phone, email, or SMS at least 24 hours in advance, as the restaurant requires. Success check: the restaurant acknowledges your confirmation and the booking stays in place.

If your plans change, transfer the reservation to another guest through Tock instead of cancelling on the day. Success check: the transfer completes in Tock and you avoid the full price same day charge.
