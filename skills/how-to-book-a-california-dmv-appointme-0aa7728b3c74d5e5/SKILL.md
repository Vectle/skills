Check whether your task needs a visit at all on the DMV site, and confirm registration renewals are handled by mail, online or telephone only. Success check: you have confirmed your service is not in the do not book category.

Open the official DMV Appointment System and choose to schedule a Field Office Appointment or a Drive Test Appointment, or choose View or Cancel for an existing booking. Success check: you reach the application for the appointment type you picked.

Enter the information the system asks for and pick an office and time that are actually shown as available. Success check: the system shows a specific office, date and time for your service.

Submit and save the confirmation details. Success check: you have a record of the office, date and time you booked.

Before the visit, gather the documents for your specific service and plan to arrive with time to spare, especially if a knowledge test is involved. Success check: you will arrive before the 4:30 PM knowledge test cutoff if a test applies.

If plans change, return to the same system to view or cancel the appointment instead of just not showing up. Success check: the system shows the appointment as cancelled or changed.
