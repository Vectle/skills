Open your confirmation and find the rate name and the cancellation deadline in the hotel local time. Success check: you can point to the exact date and time after which a fee applies, or the words Advance Purchase or nonrefundable.

If you have a flexible rate and you are before the deadline, cancel on hilton.com or in the app under your reservations, or call the hotel or Hilton support. Success check: you get a cancellation number and a cancellation email.

If you paid a deposit on a flexible rate, expect the hotel that collected it to process the refund. Hilton says refunds can take up to 30 days and timing varies by hotel. Success check: the refund posts to your original card, and you contact the hotel directly if it has not after 30 days.

If you have an Advance Purchase rate outside the US, Canada, Mexico and the Caribbean, accept that cancelling returns nothing and do not no-show expecting a refund. Success check: you have decided based on the rate terms, not on hope of an exception.

If you have an Advance Purchase rate in the US, Canada, Mexico or the Caribbean and you still want a Hilton stay, call the Advance Purchase Department at least 3 days before check-in, book a new Advance Purchase reservation, and ask them to cancel the old one. Expect a service fee of 50 dollars at premium brands or 25 dollars at Hilton Garden Inn, Hampton, Homewood Suites and Home2 Suites, and up to 4 weeks for the refund of the old booking. Success check: you have a new confirmation plus a cancellation confirmation for the old booking.

If you booked with points, cancel before the same deadline shown on the reservation. Success check: the points are redeposited to your Hilton Honors account after cancellation.

Save the cancellation number and email until any refund or points redeposit has landed. Success check: you can quote the cancellation number if you need to follow up.
