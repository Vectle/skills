Sign in to Expedia, open Trips, and open the hotel itinerary. Read the rate label and the cancellation deadline printed on that itinerary. Success check: you can say whether your rate is fully refundable or nonrefundable, and the exact deadline.

Note that most refundable hotel deadlines fall 24 to 48 hours before check in, and the deadline runs on the property clock. Success check: you have converted the deadline to your own time and still have room to spare.

Use the cancel room option on the itinerary and complete the flow, checking the refund amount shown before you confirm. Success check: the screen shows the expected refund amount, ideally the full amount for a refundable rate cancelled in time.

Save the cancellation confirmation and itinerary number. Success check: you have an email or screenshot showing cancelled status and the refund promised.

Wait for the refund to post to the original payment method. Expedia typically initiates approved refunds within about 24 hours, and banks commonly take 3 to 7 business days to show the money. Success check: the credit appears on your statement within that window.

If nothing shows after 7 business days, contact Expedia support with the itinerary number and cancellation confirmation, and ask whether the hotel or Expedia holds the funds. Success check: you get a named owner for the refund and a case reference.
