Sign in to the account that holds Premium and open the paid memberships page. Success check: your Premium membership shows there with its status and billing details.

Choose Manage membership, then Deactivate. Success check: you see both the option to continue to cancel and, on eligible plans, the option to pause instead.

To cancel, choose Continue to cancel, select a reason, then confirm with Yes, cancel. Success check: the membership status shows as cancelled with an end date.

To pause instead, choose Pause instead on the Deactivate screen, set 1 to 6 months with the slider and confirm Pause membership. Success check: the page shows the pause and the scheduled resume date, and no charge is due while paused.

If you joined from the iPhone app, cancel in your Apple account subscriptions instead, since YouTube cannot cancel Apple billed memberships. Success check: the subscription shows an expiry date in your Apple account.

If your membership comes through Google Play, cancel in your Google Play account settings. Success check: Google Play lists the subscription as cancelled.

Use Premium until the end date if you cancelled, and note the resume date if you paused. Success check: no new charge appears after the end date, or billing only restarts on the pause resume date at the then current price.
