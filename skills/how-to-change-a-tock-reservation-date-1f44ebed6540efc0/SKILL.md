Open your reservation confirmation in your Tock profile or confirmation message and read the exact cancellation policy for that booking before doing anything else. Success check: you know your venue deadline, whether rescheduling is mentioned at all, and whether the booking is free, deposit, or prepaid.

Decide which kind of booking you hold. Success check: you can say whether yours is a free reservation, which Tock says can always be cancelled through Tock, or a deposit or prepaid booking governed by the venue policy.

If your venue FAQ or confirmation offers rescheduling, follow that route inside its notice window, which varies a lot by venue, for example 48 or 72 hours at some venues and never at others. Success check: you get an updated confirmation showing the new date on the same booking.

If there is no reschedule option, cancel online from the reservation in your profile or confirmation message while you are still inside the cancellation window, then immediately book the new date as a fresh reservation. Success check: the old booking shows as cancelled and the new date is confirmed, so you are not holding two bookings or none.

If online cancellation is not offered for your booking, contact the business directly using the contact details on your confirmation, since some venues require that and Tock cannot act for them. Success check: the venue confirms the change or cancellation in writing.

If the venue will not move or refund the booking, check whether transferring is available from the reservation in your profile or via the Manage option in your confirmation, and transfer it to someone who can use the original date. Success check: the transfer is started or completed in Tock, noting only one transfer can be in progress at a time.
