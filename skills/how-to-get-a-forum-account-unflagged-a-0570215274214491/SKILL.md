## TL;DR

If your account was flagged as spam and you werent spamming, gather your evidence first: the posts that triggered it, your account age, and what you were doing when it happened. Then write the moderators one short, factual message asking for review. Most false positives get reversed in a day or two; arguing or creating a second account makes it worse.

```text
how to get a forum account unflagged after false positive
```

## Use this when

- Your account was flagged or silenced and the posts were legitimate
- An automated filter caught a new account's normal activity
- You need the appeal steps, not the ban-appeal process for real violations

## Not for this skill when

- The account actually posted spam or broke clear rules
- The account was deleted rather than flagged
- You want to dodge the flag with a new account

## Steps

1. Document what happened. Write down the flagged post titles, the date and time, and what you were doing (e.g. posting several replies in a row). Expected: a clear timeline you can paste into a message.
2. Check for a known trigger. New account plus links, rapid posting, or copy-pasted text across threads are the classic false-positive patterns. Expected: you can name the likely trigger.
3. Read the appeal channel. Most forums have a contact form, a meta category, or a moderator list for flag disputes. Expected: you find the right place to write, not a random thread.
4. Write one short factual message. State you were flagged, say what you were doing, point out the pattern looks automated, and ask for a manual review. No essays, no indignation. Expected: a message a busy moderator will actually read.
5. Wait, and pause posting. Keep posting activity on hold on that forum until the flag clears; more activity while flagged can extend it. Expected: the flag lifts in 1 to 3 days.
6. Fix the trigger afterward. If rapid posting caused it, slow down; if links did, build link-free history first. Expected: no repeat flag.

## Variant phrasings

### forum flagged my account as spam by mistake

Same process: document, identify the trigger, one factual appeal message, wait.

### how to appeal an automated spam flag

Emphasize it was automated and ask for human review; humans reverse these routinely.

### account silenced on Discourse unfairly

Discourse silences are usually automatic trust-system actions; the appeal path is the same.

## Why it happens

Spam filters are tuned for recall over precision: they would rather catch a few real users than miss a spam wave. New accounts doing new-account things (links, bursts of replies) match the spammer profile, so the system fires.

## Edge cases

- If there is no appeal channel and moderators ignore messages, the venue may not be worth the effort; see the abandon-vs-keep skill.
- A flag on one account can taint same-IP or same-email accounts; disclose related accounts if asked.
- Some forums never reverse automated flags on accounts under a week old; in that case restart the account properly rather than fighting it.
- Keep a copy of everything you send; if the venue matters, the paper trail helps on a second dispute.
