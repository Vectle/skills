## TL;DR
Old hard drives must be wiped or physically destroyed before they leave your control, with a documented chain of custody. Classify the data, choose wipe or destroy accordingly, use a certified vendor for destruction, and keep the certificates for audit.

## The query
```text
how to securely dispose of old hard drives: it asset disposal steps
```

## Use this when
- decommissioning laptops or servers with drives
- audit asks for disposal evidence
- setting up a recurring disposal process

## Not for
- personal drives (your call, but the same practices help)
- drives still under legal hold (do not destroy those)
- SSDs versus HDDs need different methods, check both

## Steps
1. Inventory every drive leaving service: serial, asset tag, and data classification. Expected output: a complete drive inventory
2. Check for legal holds or retention requirements before destroying anything. Expected output: holds cleared
3. For reuse inside the org, wipe with a certified method and verify. Expected output: verified wipe report per drive
4. For end-of-life drives, use physical destruction through a certified vendor. Expected output: destruction certificates received
5. Maintain chain of custody from collection to destruction. Expected output: an unbroken custody record
6. File the certificates where auditors can find them. Expected output: audit-ready evidence
