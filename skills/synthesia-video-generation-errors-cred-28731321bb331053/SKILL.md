## Why it happens

Synthesia knowledge base article 10104506 lists the four common video-generation errors and what each means. 'Something went wrong. Please reach out to our support team for assistance' is a general system error the support team has to investigate. 'You don't have enough credits to generate the video' means the account is out of credits. 'Convert video to template' means the video includes API variables, which only work with templates - convert the video to a template first. 'We can't generate this video because you don't have permissions for avatars in this video' means the video uses avatars or voices the account cannot use.
