Find the restaurant release pattern first. Check its OpenTable page and OpenTable toughest reservations list, which publishes per venue windows such as daily at 9 am seven days ahead for some rooms and monthly drops for others. Success check: you know the exact day and time new dates open for your venue.

Before that release moment, sign in to OpenTable, save your details, and decide your first choice and two acceptable backups in advance. Success check: at release time you can book in a few taps with no sign in or typing delay.

Book at the release moment and take the best acceptable slot you can complete, including an early or late backup if prime time vanishes. Success check: you hold a confirmed reservation, even if it is not your ideal time.

If you get nothing, set Notify me alerts right away for every date you could actually go, with the widest time range you would accept. Success check: each alert shows in your Alerts list with the right party size and time range.

Check OpenTable again the day before and on the day itself, morning and again in the afternoon, and act on any Notify me alert at once. Success check: you complete a booking and get a confirmation before the slot is taken by someone else.

Stay flexible on the final choice. Take an off peak or bar area slot if it appears rather than holding out for prime time and ending with nothing. Success check: you have a confirmed table for that night, at a time you already decided you could live with.
