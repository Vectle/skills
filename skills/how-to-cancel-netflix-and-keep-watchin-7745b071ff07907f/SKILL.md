In a web browser, sign in and open your Account page, then look at the Membership section to see who bills you. Success check: you can see either a payment method and a cancel option, meaning Netflix bills you, or the name of a payment partner.

If Netflix bills you, go to the Manage your membership page, select Cancel, then select Finish Cancellation. Success check: the page confirms your membership will end on a specific date, which is the end of your current billing period.

Check for the confirmation email Netflix sends to the account address, and note the end date shown on your Account page. Success check: you hold the confirmation and know the exact last day you can watch.

Keep watching as normal until that end date. Success check: streaming still works after cancelling, and no new Netflix charge appears after the period ends.

If a partner bills you, cancel in that partners system instead: Apple subscriptions in your device Settings, Google Play under Payments and subscriptions and Subscriptions, or your TV, internet, or mobile providers own account pages. Success check: that partner shows the Netflix subscription as cancelled with an end date.

If you paid with a gift card or promotional balance, expect access to run until that balance is used up rather than to a card billing date. Success check: your Account page reflects the balance based end timing.
