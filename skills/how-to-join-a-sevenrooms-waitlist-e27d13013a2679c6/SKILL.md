Start from the venue itself: open its website or Google listing and look for a Join the Waitlist option, or scan the QR code at the door. Success check: you land on that venue's SevenRooms waitlist page, not a generic search result.

Enter your name, party size and mobile number, plus any notes the form offers such as an occasion or allergy. Success check: the page confirms you are on the list and shows an estimated wait.

Watch for the confirmation text and keep your phone nearby. Success check: you receive a text confirming your spot, often with a link to track your place in line.

Follow the updates as the wait moves, and stay within the area the venue expects. Success check: your quoted wait updates rather than going silent.

When the table ready text arrives, reply as directed if it asks you to confirm, and head straight to the host stand. Success check: the host seats you or confirms you checked back in before the spot passes to the next party.

If your plans change, use the remove or cancel option in the text thread so the spot frees up. Success check: you get a confirmation that you were removed from the list.
