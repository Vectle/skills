Get a copy of your own agreement from Equinox Concierge if you do not have it, and find your initial period end date plus the notice period in Section 3. Success check: you can point to the exact notice period, 30 or 45 days, that your agreement states.

Work out your last effective day. Notice given 30 or more days before the end of the initial period takes effect on the last day of that period in agreements with a 30 day clause, while 45 day agreements take effect 45 days after Equinox receives notice. Success check: you have a target effective date in writing before you send anything.

If you are claiming an early exit for relocation or a medical reason, gather the proof the agreement asks for, such as proof of the move or a doctor certificate for illness or injury. Success check: your proof is ready to attach rather than promised later.

Send the cancellation using a method your agreement lists in Section 3.4: in person at a club in your membership class with the cancellation form handed to the general manager or assistant general manager, certified or registered mail to the Concierge address printed in the agreement, email from your address on file to the cancellation or concierge address printed in the agreement, phone through Concierge where allowed after the initial period, or online in account settings where offered. Include your full name, membership ID number, and contact information. Success check: you hold a dated copy, a mail receipt, or a sent email you can produce later.

Ask for confirmation of the effective date and the final billing date, and keep paying through the notice period since dues run until the cancellation takes effect. Success check: you have the effective date confirmed, not just an acknowledgement that your message arrived.

Check your payment method after the effective date to confirm charges have stopped. Success check: no new dues post after the confirmed final billing date.
