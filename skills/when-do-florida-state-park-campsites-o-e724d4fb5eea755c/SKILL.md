Work out your window from your arrival date. Count back 11 months if you are a Florida resident, 10 months if you are not. Success check: you have a calendar date for the morning your site opens.

Pick a first choice park and site type plus one backup park before release day, and create your reservation account with payment ready. Success check: you can log in and see the park and your dates without errors.

Be on the Florida State Parks reservation site a few minutes before 8 a.m. Eastern on release day and search the moment new sites appear. Success check: sites for your arrival date show as bookable instead of outside the window.

Complete the booking and pay in advance, including the nonrefundable reservation fee per transaction. Success check: you get a confirmation for the exact park, site, and dates.

If your first choice is gone, book the backup park or a weekday version of the trip right away rather than refreshing for hours. Success check: you hold a confirmed site somewhere you would actually stay.
