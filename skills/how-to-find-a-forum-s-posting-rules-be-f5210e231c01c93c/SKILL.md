## TL;DR

A forum's posting rules are usually findable without an account: check the nav for Guidelines or Rules, read the pinned threads in the main categories, and read the fine print on the signup page itself. Ten minutes of reading before signup saves weeks of warming a venue whose rules you were going to break. The things that matter most are the link policy and the AI-content policy.

```text
how to find a forum's posting rules before signing up
```

## Use this when

- You are evaluating a new forum venue before signing up
- You want the link, signature, and AI-content rules up front
- You are building a venue shortlist and need per-forum rule notes

## Not for this skill when

- You already have an account and need to appeal a violation
- The forum requires login to view anything (rare; note it and move on)
- You want the rules of a private community you cant see

## Steps

1. Check the nav and footer. Look for Guidelines, Rules, FAQ, Code of Conduct, or Terms. Expected: the primary rules page in one or two clicks.
2. Read the pinned threads. Open the top pinned posts in the main help or meta category; working rules often live there, not on the rules page. Expected: category-specific norms the rules page omits.
3. Read the signup page fine print. The registration form often links to terms with extra clauses about accounts, automation, and commercial posting. Expected: the legal-level rules before you agree to them.
4. Extract the four key answers. Link policy for new users, AI or bot content policy, signature rules, self-promotion rules. Expected: a four-line note per venue.
5. Check the dates. Rules pages go stale; look for a last-updated date or recent staff edits. Expected: you know whether the rules reflect current enforcement.
6. Note the gaps. If the rules say nothing about AI content or links, write that down too; silence is information. Expected: a complete rule note, including what wasnt said.

## Variant phrasings

### where to find forum guidelines before registering

Nav, footer, pinned threads, signup-page terms: same four stops.

### how to read a forum's rules for link posting

Search the rules page for link, URL, promotion, and spam; then check what new-user restrictions the pinned newbie threads mention.

### forum terms of service what to look for

Automation, multiple accounts, commercial content, and content licensing clauses are the ones that bite agents.

## Why it happens

Forums scatter their rules across pages written at different times by different people: the friendly guidelines, the legal terms, and the pinned moderator threads each carry part of the real policy. Reading only one of them gives you an incomplete picture.

## Edge cases

- Some forums keep rules in a wiki or a docs site linked from the footer; follow one hop out.
- Non-English forums may have rules only in the local language; machine-translate the page rather than guessing.
- If you genuinely cannot find any rules, treat the venue as strict and post conservatively.
- Rules behind login are a red flag for automation; note it in the venue record.
