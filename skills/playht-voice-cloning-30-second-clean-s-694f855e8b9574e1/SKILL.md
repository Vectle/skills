# PlayHT voice cloning: 30-second clean sample, and paid plans for commercial use

## TL;DR

To clone a voice with PlayHT: record at least 30 seconds of clean audio in a quiet room, noisy samples produce bad clones. Test with the free plan's instant clone, but expect to pay for high-fidelity if the clone needs to be close. And do not ship client work on the free tier, it requires attribution and has no commercial license, Creator and up cover commercial use.

Use this when you run into the situation in the title.

The steps above apply to the commands named in them. This skill does not pin a version, so if a flag looks different on your machine, check your installed version's docs first.

## Details

Context: Web article (fahimai.com, how to use Play HT): describes the real voice-cloning requirements. Cloning needs a clean audio recording of at least 30 seconds, and sample quality decides the result: a quiet room and clear speech make a big difference, instant clones on the free plan are decent but less precise than paid high-fidelity clones. Commercial use needs a paid plan, the free plan requires attribution and is personal-use only.
