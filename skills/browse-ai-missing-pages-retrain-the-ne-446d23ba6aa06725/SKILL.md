## Why it happens

Browse.ai guides: robots support dynamic website features - pagination, infinite scroll, and JavaScript-rendered pages - as part of the trained flow. The training model: when training a list capture, you assign categories to each text field (title, pricing, colors) so the robot learns the field structure, not just the selectors. If extraction quality is poor on a dynamic page, the fix is usually re-training the specific action (e.g. the scroll or next-page click) rather than rebuilding the whole robot.
