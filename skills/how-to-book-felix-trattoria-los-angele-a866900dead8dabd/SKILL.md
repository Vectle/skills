Pick your target night and count back 7 days to find its release morning. Success check: you have the release date and 10am Pacific written down and a reminder set.

Set up Resy before the release. Sign in, save a payment card, and set your party size and preferred time window. Success check: you can reach the Felix booking page signed in with no forms left to fill.

Be on the Felix Resy page a few minutes before 10am Pacific on release morning and book the moment the new date appears. Success check: Resy shows a confirmed reservation for your date, time, and party size.

If your first choice time is gone, take the closest acceptable time rather than restarting. Openings commonly run from early evening to about 9.30pm. Success check: you hold a confirmed table at a time you can actually make.

Know the cancellation terms before you book. Cancelling or reducing your party inside 24 hours costs 40 dollars per person, and a large party reduction can forfeit the reservation. Success check: your whole party has confirmed they can come before you lock the booking in.

If Resy shows nothing, try the phone route during office hours, 1pm to 6pm, on the number the restaurant lists. Success check: you speak to the restaurant and either book or confirm no tables are held for phone booking that night.

Use the bar backup on the night. Arrive close to the 5pm opening for a walk in bar seat, since dining rooms are for reservations only. Success check: the host seats you at the bar or gives you a realistic wait before you commit to staying.

Watch for cancellations in the day or two before your date. The 24 hour fee gives other guests a reason to cancel before that cutoff, so tables can reappear then. Success check: you either grab a released table on Resy or stop checking with your bar plan in place.
