Check each line for a device payment balance in My Verizon, under Device Overview. Verizon says you must pay off outstanding device payment balances before disconnecting the associated lines, and any balance not paid separately lands on the final bill. Success check: you have a payoff amount written down for every line you plan to move or close.

If you are keeping a number, generate a Number Transfer PIN in My Verizon as the account owner or account manager, and turn off Number Lock if it is on. Verizon lists PIN generation through the My Verizon website or app, or by dialing the port request code from the phone on the line. Success check: you have the PIN and your Verizon account number ready to give only to the new carrier.

Start the move with the new carrier, not with Verizon. Give the new carrier the numbers to move, the account number, and the transfer PIN. The new carrier contacts Verizon, transfers the number, and disconnects the line. Success check: the number works on the new carrier and you can make and receive calls on it.

If you are closing lines without moving them, sign in to My Verizon, choose Chat with us, type Cancel in the question box, and follow the prompts to disconnect a line or chat with a live agent, or call Customer Service with the account owner name, phone number, and account PIN ready. Success check: you get a confirmation for each line and a note of the billing period end date.

Wait for the account to close correctly. If you moved every number to a new carrier, Verizon says the account closes automatically and you do not need to contact them. If you took new numbers instead, the account does not close automatically and you must close it yourself. Success check: the final lines show as disconnected in My Verizon.

Pay and file the final bill. It covers usage, taxes, and surcharges through the termination date, any remaining device balance in full, and stopped promotional credits, while security deposits, if any, are processed within 30 to 60 days. You can still sign in with the account owner user ID to view the bill after disconnection. Success check: the final bill matches the payoff amounts you noted in step 1, and you have saved a copy.
