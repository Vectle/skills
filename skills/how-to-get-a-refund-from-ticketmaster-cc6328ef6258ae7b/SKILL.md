Check the event status in your Ticketmaster account and in the notice Ticketmaster sent you. Work out which bucket you are in: canceled, or postponed, rescheduled or moved. Success check: the status in your account matches the notice, and you know the organizer for the event.

If the event is canceled, do nothing and wait. Ticketmaster's help page says no action is required, the refund goes to the original payment method as soon as funds are received from the Event Organizer, and it should appear within 14 to 21 days depending on your bank, with exceptions noted for MLB games, the Masters and the US Open Tennis Championships. Success check: the refund lands on the original payment method, or you have the order number ready to chase it after that window.

If the event is postponed, rescheduled or moved, keep the tickets unless the organizer opens a choice. Ticketmaster says tickets stay valid for the new date and no further action is required. If the organizer approves a refund or a choice between a refund and a credit, Ticketmaster will notify you. Success check: you can see the new date in your account, and you know whether a refund choice was offered.

If a refund choice was offered, use the Request Refund button in your order before the deadline in the notice. Ticketmaster's help guidance says that if the organizer has approved refunds, you will see that button in your account. Success check: you get an on screen or email confirmation that the request was submitted for the right tickets.

If you sold tickets fan to fan and the event is then canceled, expect the sale to unwind. Ticketmaster says it will charge the card on file to refund the buyer, put the tickets back on your account, and then refund you to your original payment method once organizer funds arrive. Success check: the tickets are back in your account and both legs of the money movement are explained in your notices.

If no refund button appears and the event is not canceled, stop and use the remaining options instead: keep the tickets for the new date, transfer them, or list them for resale if resale is open for that event. Success check: you have not missed a stated deadline while waiting for a button that was never offered.
