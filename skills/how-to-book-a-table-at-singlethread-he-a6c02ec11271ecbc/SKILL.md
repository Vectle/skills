Set up your OpenTable account with payment details saved in advance. Success check: checkout on release morning needs no card typing.

Mark the release: 9:00 AM Pacific on the 1st of the month, for the following month (April 1 opens May, for example). Success check: a reminder is set for 8:45 AM that day.

At the release, book your date and prepay in full. Success check: OpenTable shows the reservation confirmed and paid; anything short of payment is not a booking.

If the dining room is gone, price the inn route: rooms book up to 12 weeks ahead and every inn stay carries a guaranteed restaurant reservation for your party, with breakfast included. Success check: you have compared the room cost against another month of failed drops.

Join the restaurant newsletter list so the monthly opening lands in your inbox. Success check: you are subscribed before the next 1st.

If plans change, transfer the booking from your OpenTable profile instead of eating the cost. Success check: the new guest holds the reservation under their own profile.
