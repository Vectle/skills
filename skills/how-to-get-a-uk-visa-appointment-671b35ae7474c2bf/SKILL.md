Finish the online application and pay the fee. GOV.UK says the fee is paid as part of the online application, and the appointment booking happens as part of the application process. Success check: you have your application reference and the link or route into the centre booking system for your country.

Check whether the ID Check app applies to you before booking a trip to a centre. GOV.UK says you find out when you start the application if you can use the app instead. Success check: you know for certain whether you need an in person appointment.

Look for a free appointment first. GOV.UK says most visa application centres offer free appointments within 5 working days. Success check: you have viewed the free slots at your nearest centre before considering any paid option.

Compare the paid local slot against travelling. GOV.UK says that in some locations you will need to pay 76.50 pounds for a standard appointment, and that you can choose to travel to a different centre with free appointments if you pay your own travel costs. Success check: you have compared the fee against the travel cost and time for the nearest free centre.

Book inside the deadline. GOV.UK says you must book and attend an appointment within 240 days of submitting your application, or you will need to start a new application. Success check: your booked date sits well inside that window.

Decide on optional services with clear eyes. GOV.UK says you can pay for optional services such as getting your documents scanned for you, and that paying for optional services does not make your application more likely to succeed. Success check: you only pay for an extra you actually need, such as scanning you cannot do yourself.

Attend with the right people and documents. The person applying must attend to give biometrics and documents, children under 16 must go with an adult over 18, and children under 5 attend for a photograph only, with no fingerprints. Success check: everyone who must attend is booked in and travelling together.
