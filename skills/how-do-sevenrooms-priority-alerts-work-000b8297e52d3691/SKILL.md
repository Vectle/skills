Try to book your preferred date, time, and party size on the venue booking page as normal. Success check: the venue tells you that exact request is not available.

Set a Priority Alert for that request when the venue offers it. Success check: you are registered to be notified if a table becomes available for that date, time, or party size.

Make sure the venue can reach you by text message or email, the two channels SevenRooms says Priority Alerts use. Success check: your contact details on the request are current and notifications are not blocked.

Wait for an alert and act right away if one arrives. Success check: you open the alert and complete a booking, an alert alone is not a reservation.

Keep a backup plan while you wait. Success check: you have another date, time, or venue in mind if no matching table opens or someone ahead of you claims it first.
