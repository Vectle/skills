Create or sign in to your Resy account in advance and save your party size and payment details if prompted. Success check: you can open The Grill page on Resy while signed in.

Count back 30 days from your target date and set an alarm for just before 10 a.m. Eastern that day. Success check: you know the exact morning your date should open.

At the release, search dinner first if that is your goal and book any workable prime time immediately. Success check: Resy shows a confirmed reservation for your date and party size.

If prime dinner is gone, search the same date for weekday lunch, Monday to Friday 11:45 a.m. to 2 p.m. per the restaurant site, then search 5 p.m. and after 9 p.m. dinner seatings. Success check: you find a bookable lunch or off prime slot on the same date.

If nothing is open, turn on Resy Notify for your date range and check back for cancellations in the final week. Success check: Notify is active for at least one date you can actually attend.
