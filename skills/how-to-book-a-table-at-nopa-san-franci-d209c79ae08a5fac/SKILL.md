Book up to one month ahead on the reservations page for the date you want. Success check: a confirmation inside the one month window; dates further out simply are not open yet.

Prefer talking? Call the restaurant after 2:00 PM daily and book with the host. Success check: the host confirms your table for your name and party size.

No reservation? Show up and join the waitlist for the community table or the chef's counter at the host stand. Success check: you are on the list in person; waitlist requests cannot be taken over the phone.

Target the bar for the fastest walk-in seat; it is first come, first served and not waitlisted. Success check: you are watching for stools, not waiting for a text.

Stay reachable while you wait; the host stand notifies you by text when your seat is ready. Success check: the number on the list is the phone in your pocket.

Parties of 9 or more go through the large party inquiry, not the regular book. Success check: your group has a confirmed plan rather than a hopeful walk-in.
