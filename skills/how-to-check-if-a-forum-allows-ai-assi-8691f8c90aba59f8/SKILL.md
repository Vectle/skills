## TL;DR

Before you post anything AI-assisted on a forum, find its actual written stance: check the rules page, terms of service, and any staff posts about AI or automation, in that order. If nothing is written, the safe read is no, and you ask a moderator. Getting this wrong gets accounts banned, so the check takes ten minutes and is always worth it.

```text
how to check if a forum allows AI-assisted replies
```

## Use this when

- You are about to sign up for a forum where you might post AI-assisted replies
- You want to know a community's stance on AI content before investing effort
- You are building a venue list and need a yes/no/maybe per forum

## Not for this skill when

- The forum already told you directly what is allowed
- You want to challenge or appeal an AI-content ban
- You are looking for places to post AI content that bans it

## Steps

1. Find the rules page. Look for Guidelines, Rules, FAQ, or Code of Conduct in the nav or footer. Expected: a page stating what is and isnt allowed.
2. Search the rules for AI terms. Use the page search for AI, artificial intelligence, bot, automated, generated. Expected: an explicit clause, or nothing.
3. Check the terms of service. Search the ToS for the same terms; bans often live in the legal text rather than the friendly guidelines. Expected: a ToS-level prohibition if one exists.
4. Search staff posts. Use the forum search for AI from staff or moderator accounts; policy clarifications usually land in announcement threads. Expected: the current enforcement posture, which can be newer than the rules page.
5. Check for a disclosure norm. Some forums allow AI-assisted replies if labeled; look for how others disclose it. Expected: you know whether disclosure makes it acceptable.
6. If silent, ask first. Post in the meta or introductions category asking about AI-assisted reply policy before posting any. Expected: a written answer you can point to later.

## Variant phrasings

### does this forum allow AI-generated posts

Same check. AI-generated and AI-assisted are treated the same by most moderators.

### forum policy on bots and automation

Run the same three-document check: rules, ToS, staff posts. Bot-specific rules sometimes differ from AI-content rules.

### how to tell if a community bans AI content

Look for phrases like no AI-generated content, no bot accounts, or original content only in the rules or recent staff announcements.

## Why it happens

Many forums added AI-content rules in the last couple of years after waves of low-effort AI spam. The rules live in different places on different forums, and enforcement often runs ahead of the written policy, which is why staff posts matter.

## Edge cases

- A forum may allow AI assistance for drafting but require human review; the check still works, you just interpret a yes as conditional.
- Policies change; a green light from last year is worth re-checking if you paused the venue.
- Some forums ban AI content in help categories but allow it in off-topic; check category-specific rules too.
- Private or invite-only forums may state policy during onboarding instead of on a public page.
