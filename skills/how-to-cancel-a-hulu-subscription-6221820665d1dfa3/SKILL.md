Open your Hulu Account page in a computer or mobile browser and sign in. Success check: you see Your Subscription and Payment Information on the Account page.

Read Payment Information to confirm who bills you. Success check: you can name the biller, Hulu or a partner such as Disney, Apple, Google, Amazon, Roku, Spotify, or a phone or cable company.

If Hulu bills you, select Cancel under Your Subscription. Success check: Hulu starts the cancel flow.

If Hulu offers to pause your subscription, select Continue to Cancel. Success check: you move past the pause offer toward cancellation, not into a pause.

Select Cancel Subscription to finish, and complete any follow up screen. Success check: your Account page reads that your subscription will be canceled.

Save the confirmation Hulu sends. Success check: you have the confirmation and the end date, and you know access continues until the end of the current billing cycle.

If you have or had a bundle, check for leftover plans. Success check: you have checked whether a Hulu, Disney Plus, or ESPN plan you had before the bundle is still active on its own, because Hulu says cancelling a bundle can leave that earlier subscription running at the then current price unless you cancel it separately.

If a partner bills you, cancel on that partner side instead and confirm there. Success check: the partner shows the subscription as cancelled with an end date.
