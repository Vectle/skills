Work out your vehicle type before you book. Standard is 17 feet or shorter, medium is over 17 feet and shorter than 22 feet, and large is 22 to 35 feet. Pick Accessible if you have an ADA placard, and Electric Vehicle only if you want a charging space. Success check: your vehicle length matches the reservation type you are about to buy, since arriving in the wrong type can get you turned away.

Set a booking alarm for 9:00am Pacific Time, 90 days before your visit date, when reservations are released on a rolling basis. If that date has already passed, check availability right away and also check the smaller release 3 days ahead. Success check: GoMuirWoods shows your date with at least one arrival window still open.

On GoMuirWoods, or by phone at 1-800-410-2419, choose your date, vehicle type, and 30 minute arrival window, then pay. Standard parking is 10 dollars, medium is 30 dollars, large is 45 dollars, and the shuttle is 4 dollars per adult round trip. Visitors age 16 or older also pay a 15 dollar park entrance fee unless a pass covers it. Success check: you reach a confirmation with a barcode pass or a phone confirmation number.

Download or print the pass while you still have signal, and save a screenshot as a backup. Success check: the pass opens on your phone with no connection turned on, because there is no cell reception or public WiFi at Muir Woods to fetch it later.

Arrive inside your 30 minute arrival window. Standard, accessible, and electric vehicle reservations let you stay until the end of park hours that day. Medium and large reservations are different, since you must arrive and leave inside a 1 hour 45 minute block. Success check: a parking ambassador scans your pass and directs you to a space.

If parking is sold out, switch to the shuttle instead of driving up anyway. The shuttle serves the Larkspur Ferry Terminal, runs on weekends and holidays from April through October, and needs its own reservation. Success check: you hold a shuttle reservation for a date and time when the shuttle is actually operating.
