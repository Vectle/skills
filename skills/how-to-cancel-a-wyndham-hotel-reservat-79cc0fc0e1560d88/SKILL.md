Open your confirmation and find the Cancellation and Rate Details, noting the free cancellation cutoff in hotel local time and whether the rate was prepaid. Success check: you can state your exact cutoff and whether money has already been taken.

Go to Find Reservation on wyndhamhotels.com to view or cancel, or sign in as a Wyndham Rewards member and open My Reservations under My Account, then cancel the stay. Success check: the reservation shows as cancelled and you keep the confirmation.

On a flexible rate cancelled in time, check that no charge follows, since Wyndham says flexible rate cards are not charged at booking and payment happens at the hotel. If a deposit was taken, Wyndham says a refund can take up to 30 days to reflect depending on bank timelines. Success check: no unexpected charge appears, or a deposit refund lands within that window.

On a Pay Now and Save booking, do not cancel expecting a refund. The rate terms say prepaid, non-cancellable and non-refundable, with the total room charge plus taxes forfeited on cancellation or modification. Success check: you decide to keep or forfeit the booking with the full cost understood.

For a Wyndham Rewards free night, cancel by the earlier of 4:00 pm the day before check-in in the hotel time zone and the property deadline. Wyndham terms say points return automatically when you cancel in line with the property policy, and are forfeited if you cancel outside it, no-show, or if the points had already expired. Success check: the points are back in your account and none were expired points that would be lost.

If you cannot cancel online, contact the hotel directly and ask them to cancel under the policy on your confirmation. Success check: the hotel confirms cancellation and the fee position in writing or by confirmation.
