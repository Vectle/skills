Start at cotognasf.com and follow its reservation flow, which hands you to the restaurant's SevenRooms page. Success check: you are on Cotogna's SevenRooms page with date and party size selectors, not on a third party listing.

Work out your release moment: reservations open one month in advance to the calendar day at 12:00 AM Pacific, so a table for the 18th of next month becomes bookable at midnight on the 18th of this month. Success check: the release date and time are in your calendar with a reminder the evening before.

Book at the release for prime dinner times, above all Friday and Saturday nights. Success check: a SevenRooms confirmation showing the date, time, and party size you wanted, or a fallback you chose on purpose.

If dinner is gone, take lunch instead: lunch runs Wednesday through Saturday from 11:30 AM and is far easier to land than dinner. Success check: a lunch confirmation in hand rather than another month of refreshing.

Party of 6 to 12: skip the standard reservation flow and use the group dining route on the restaurant's site, which covers the Chef's Table and the private outdoor terrace. Success check: the group dining side is handling your party, not the public widget.

Check the calendar shape before you plan around a date: Cotogna is closed on Sundays, serves dinner only on Monday and Tuesday (from 4:30 PM), and lunch plus dinner Wednesday through Saturday. Success check: your target date is a day and meal the restaurant actually serves.

## Community-reported tips (not the restaurant's published policy)

The tips below come from diner reports and local guides, not from the restaurant. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- Bar and counter seats are held for walk-ins, and the full menu is served there (multiple 2026 sources agree), so a bar seat is the same meal, not a downgrade.
- Arriving right at opening works: firsthand reports include bar seats immediately on a Tuesday at open and a walk-in party seated at a table on a Saturday around 4:30 PM. If the bar is full, reported waits run about 20 to 45 minutes, with the host stand giving come-back times.
- Weekday lunch (Wednesday to Saturday from 11:30 AM) is the soft spot, with mostly the same menu; measured data shows most surfaced openings are before 6 PM.
- Cancelled tables trickle back in the last few days before the date, so re-checking SevenRooms close in is worthwhile.
