Make a Recreation.gov account well before February 1 and confirm you can log in. Success check: you can open the Mt. Whitney permit page on Recreation.gov while signed in.

Between February 1 and March 1, submit one lottery application for day use, pick your preferred date plus as many alternate dates and alternate leaders as the form allows, and pay the application fee shown at checkout. Success check: the application shows in your Recreation.gov account.

On March 15, log in and check your lottery application for the result. Success check: you can see a clear won or not won status for each choice.

If you won, complete the reservation details and pay the 15 dollars per person fee before 9 p.m. Pacific on April 21. Success check: you have a paid reservation confirmation, not just a lottery win notice.

Print or save your permit by the deadline in your confirmation, which for day use is noon the day before your hike. Success check: you hold the permit itself and can show it on the trail.

If you did not win, be logged in before 7 a.m. Pacific on April 22 with backup dates ready and book an unclaimed date first come, first served. Success check: the date completes checkout and appears as a reservation in your account.

If April 22 fails too, keep checking the Recreation.gov calendar for your target window through the season, since cancelled permits return to the system. Success check: any date you grab completes checkout the same way.
