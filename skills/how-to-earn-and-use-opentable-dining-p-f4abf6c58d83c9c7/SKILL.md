Create an OpenTable account if you need one, sign in, and join OpenTable Regulars if it is offered in your region. Success check: you see a Rewards or Points area in your account.

Search in the OpenTable app or on opentable.com while signed in and check the points shown before you confirm. Standard tables show 100 points and bonus point tables show a higher amount up to 1000 points. Success check: your confirmation shows the reservation is points eligible and how many points it can earn.

Go to the reservation and dine as booked. Do not cancel and do not no show. Success check: the restaurant marks the reservation as seated or completed in OpenTable.

Wait for the points to post and check them under Rewards, then Points history. OpenTable says points usually appear within about 7 days. Success check: the visit moves from pending to earned in your points history.

Redeem once you have enough points. In the United States, 1000 points gets 8 dollars toward a prepaid experience, 2000 points gets 16 dollars toward a reservation or a prepaid experience or 10 dollars as an Amazon gift card, 5000 points gets 40 dollars in dining or experience credit or 25 dollars as an Amazon gift card, and 10000 points gets 80 dollars in dining or experience credit or 50 dollars as an Amazon gift card. Success check: before you confirm, the Rewards screen shows the points cost and the reward value for your region.

Use a dining credit the way OpenTable requires. Book the new reservation through the dining credit or Dining Reward flow instead of trying to apply the credit to a reservation you already booked. Success check: the new reservation confirmation shows the credit attached.

Watch expiry. In the United States, unredeemed points expire three years after they were earned, described by OpenTable as within three years from the end of the calendar quarter in which they were collected, and all points expire if the account has no seated reservation for 12 months. Success check: the Rewards hub shows your next expiry amount and date and you have a plan to use or keep earning before then.
