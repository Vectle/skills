Mark December 1 of the year before your trip, so December 1, 2026 for a summer 2027 visit, and decide your campground and dates before that morning. Success check: you know which campground, which site type, and which dates you will book the moment reservations open.

Go to the reservation system linked from the NPS Denali campgrounds page, ReserveDenali, run by the concessioner Doyon and ARAMARK Joint Venture. Success check: you are booking through the concessioner site NPS links to, not a general camping marketplace.

Book your campground and site category for your full stay, keeping the season in mind. Most campgrounds are summer only, Teklanika and Sanctuary and Igloo run May 20 to mid September, and Wonder Lake runs June 8 to mid September, while Riley Creek is open year round. Success check: your dates fall inside your campground's published season.

If you are driving to Teklanika, book at least 3 nights, plan on one vehicle per site, and expect to leave the vehicle parked at camp while you explore by bus. Success check: your Teklanika reservation is 3 nights or longer and your plans do not depend on driving in and out.

Note the park wide limits before you finalize, a 14 night maximum for the summer season, check in and check out at 11:00 am, and no hookups at any campground. Success check: your stay is within 14 nights and your vehicle is 40 feet or under, the largest any Denali campground takes.

Check in on arrival at the Riley Creek Mercantile or the Denali Bus Depot, then pick any open site of the type you reserved inside your campground. Success check: you are checked in and choosing among available sites of your reserved type, rather than looking for an assigned site number.
