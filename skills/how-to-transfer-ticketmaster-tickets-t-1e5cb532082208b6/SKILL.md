Sign in to the Ticketmaster account that holds the order and open My Tickets. Success check: you can see the correct event, date, and seats.

Select Transfer to check the order is eligible. You may get a text with a one time code to verify your identity, so use the phone number on your account. If you no longer have access to that number, contact Fan Support before going further. Success check: the ticket selection screen opens instead of a message that the organiser has not activated transfer.

Select only the tickets you want to send and choose Transfer To. Keep your own ticket unselected if you are still going. Success check: the count and seat details match what you intend to send.

Enter the recipient contact information. Ticketmaster recommends sending to the email address tied to the recipient Ticketmaster account. Text transfer is available in the app or mobile browser for some events, and mobile browser users may also see a WhatsApp option, in which case you must actually press send in the message app. Success check: the name and contact details are spelled exactly as the recipient uses them.

Confirm the transfer. Success check: the tickets show as sent in your account, with a status such as Waiting to accept or Sent.

Make sure the recipient accepts. They tap the link in the text, email, or WhatsApp message, sign in or create a Ticketmaster account using the same email the tickets were sent to for email transfers, and check the tickets appear in My Tickets. Success check: your account shows Accepted by or Claimed, and the recipient can open the ticket in their own account before event day.
