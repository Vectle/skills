Open the restaurant on Resy or on its business page and look for Join Walk In Waitlist for today. Success check: the option appears for today at that venue, which confirms the venue has the feature enabled.

Join with your party details and phone number. Success check: you receive a text message confirming you have been added to the waitlist.

Head to the restaurant and stay reachable by text. Wait times are worked out by the venue team, not set automatically by the app. Success check: you can see or confirm you are listed as a walk in online party, separate from parties the host added in person.

If your plans change, leave the list by texting 9 as the official FAQ instructs. Success check: you get confirmation you have been removed, and the venue is not holding a spot for you.

If there is no online join option, go to the host stand in person and ask to be added as a regular walk in. Success check: the host confirms your name and party size are on their list and gives you their own estimate or text process.
