# how to get a reservation at Quince San Francisco

## TL;DR

Quince releases reservations on the 1st of the month prior for the whole following month, and booking runs through SevenRooms from the restaurant's own site: July tables, for example, open on June 1. The Gastronomy menu is served in the main dining room, the bar and salon run a shorter four-course menu, and lunch is offered Friday and Saturday only.

## Steps

1. Work out your release date: the 1st of the month before the month you want. Success check: it is in your calendar with a morning reminder.
2. On release day, book through the reservation link on Quince's own site, which hands you to SevenRooms. Success check: a SevenRooms confirmation, not just a calendar hold.
3. Choose the room deliberately: the main dining room for the full Gastronomy menu, or the bar and salon for the four-course California Coast and Valleys menu. Success check: the experience named on your confirmation matches the meal you wanted.
4. Missed your date? Keep checking SevenRooms as the date approaches; month-ahead releases shed tables as plans change. Success check: you have checked again inside the final week, not only on release day.
5. For a group, ask about the private dining room above the bar, which seats up to 16. Success check: your inquiry is with the private dining side, not stuck in the public queue.

## When to use

You are planning a month or more ahead and want the full tasting menu experience.

## When not to use

Same-week plans. The monthly release means the current month's tables were decided weeks ago.

## Edge cases

- The restaurant closes on some holidays (Thanksgiving and Christmas in 2026), so those dates never release.
- Special event dinners, like a Christmas Eve lunch or a New Years Eve dinner, are listed as separate experiences in SevenRooms and go first.
- Lunch exists only on Friday and Saturday, as a four-course menu.

## Community-reported tips (not the restaurant's published policy)

Diners, reviewers and booking trackers report the following. None of it is published by the restaurant, and any of it can change without notice.

1. Seats in the Bolinas Bar are first come, first served if you are not booking the four-course menu there, so walking in at the bar is a real route on a night when the dining room is gone.
2. Thursdays are the easiest night of the week in tracker data URIs a table for two showed in 55 percent of checks, against 36 percent on Fridays. Tables for four appear on more dates than tables for two, so adding another couple can be the difference.
3. Openings skew early. The seating that comes back most often is 5:00 PM, and nothing in the tracker record surfaces after 9 PM, so do not hold out for a late table that is not coming.
