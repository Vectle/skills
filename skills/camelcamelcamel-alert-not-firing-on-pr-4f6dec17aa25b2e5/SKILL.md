Open your price watches and find the product, then read the desired price and the price type shown on that watch.

Open the product page on CamelCamelCamel and look at the price history chart for that same price type.

Check whether the watch is set to alert at your desired price or only on any drop, depending on the option you chose at creation.

Confirm the alert delivery setting on your account and check spam or promotions folders for the alert message.

Delete the watch and recreate it at the current price with a threshold a small amount below it, then wait for the next recorded price change.

If the chart shows the drop and the watch was correct, use the product page report or support contact to flag the missed alert with the date of the drop.
