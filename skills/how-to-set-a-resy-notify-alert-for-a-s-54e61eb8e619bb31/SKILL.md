Search the restaurant in the Resy app or on resy.com, enter your date and party size, and confirm the date is truly sold out. If any time is bookable, book it instead of waiting on alerts.

Tap Notify, the bell icon, for that date and set your preferred times. Choose the full range you would take, for example 5 pm to 10 pm rather than only 7 pm. Success check: the date shows as a Notify request in your account.

Repeat for every alternate date you would accept. Each date is its own Notify request, and more dates means more cancellation waves you can catch.

Enable push notifications in the app and allow them on your lock screen. Email alerts alone are usually too slow for popular rooms.

Save a payment card to your Resy account ahead of time. Many restaurants require a card to complete a booking, and typing one in while a table sits open is how tables get lost.

When an alert lands, tap through and book immediately, then check for the confirmation email. No confirmation email means you did not get the table.

## Community-reported tips (not Resy's published policy)

The tips below come from diner reports and reservation guides, not from Resy. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- Even with Notify set, check the app yourself at the cancellation deadline hour, 24 or 48 hours before your date. That is when the cancellation wave lands, and the alert can lag behind it.
- A party of two with a wide time range converts far more openings than a party of four fixed on one prime time; the first and last seatings of the night are the times that surface most.
- If you hold an eligible American Express Platinum or Centurion card, linking it to your Resy account turns on Priority Notify at participating restaurants, and those alerts go out before standard ones.
