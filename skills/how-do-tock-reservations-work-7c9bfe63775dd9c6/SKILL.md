Open the venue page on Tock and open the specific experience, not just the venue calendar. Success check: you can see the experience name, date, time, and party size options.

Identify the payment type before checkout. Look for prepaid in full, a deposit amount, a card hold, or no payment. Success check: you know exactly how much is charged today and how much, if anything, is due at the venue.

Read the cancellation and transfer policy shown for that experience. Success check: you know the deadline for any refund and whether transfers are allowed.

Check the total, including any add ons you select at checkout, such as pairings or extras. Success check: the checkout total matches what you expected to pay today.

Complete the purchase to secure the ticket. Success check: the booking appears in your Tock profile and you get a confirmation with the business contact details.

Manage the booking from your Tock profile. For a free reservation, cancel through Tock if plans change. For a paid booking, follow the business policy in your confirmation. Success check: any change shows as updated in your profile.

If you cannot go and the booking allows it, transfer the reservation through Tock to another person for up to the original purchase price, per Tock guest help. Success check: the new guest accepts the transfer and the booking shows under their name.
