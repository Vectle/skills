Open the gift card delivery message and accept the gift card while signed in to the Tock account you will book with, creating an account first if you need one. Success check: the gift card is attached to your account instead of sitting unclaimed in an inbox.

Open your Tock profile while logged in and note your gift card balance before you shop. Success check: you can see a balance in your profile, which is where Tock says balances are shown.

Pick an experience where payment is due at booking with a participating business, since the balance only shows at checkout for participating businesses. Success check: the experience shows a price due now, not just a hold or a pay at the restaurant setup.

Go to checkout and confirm your gift card balance is applied by default, applied up to the full cost of the purchase if the balance allows it. Success check: the amount due drops by your balance before you pay anything.

Pay any amount above your balance with a payment method Tock accepts at that moment. Success check: the booking confirms and the split between gift card and card payment is shown.

Reopen your profile after booking to confirm the remaining balance, if any. Success check: the new balance matches the old balance minus what checkout applied.
