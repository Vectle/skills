## TL;DR

Finding new directories is an ongoing habit, not a one-time search: set up recurring searches for "[niche] tools directory" and "submit [niche] tool", watch where newly launched competitors get listed, and note directories mentioned in communities and newsletters. Review the prospect list monthly and promote the good ones to your submission queue.

```text
how to find new directories in your niche
```

## Use this when

- The initial directory list is exhausted
- Entering or tracking a niche over time
- Keeping a submission pipeline fed

## Not for this skill when

- You have not worked the existing list yet (finish it first)
- The niche is tiny and fully mapped (accept it)
- You want bulk additions without vetting (quality over quantity)

## Steps

1. Set up recurring searches. Monthly, run your core directory-discovery queries and check for new results. Expected: new candidates surface over time as directories launch.

2. Watch competitor launches. When a competitor or adjacent tool launches, note where it gets listed in its first month; those directories accept new tools. Expected: fresh, proven-accepting targets.

3. Monitor communities and newsletters. Niche newsletters and community threads mention new directories before search engines index them. Expected: early awareness of new venues.

4. Vet before adding. Check the directory is maintained, relevant, and accepts tools like yours. Expected: every prospect earns its place on the list.

```
prospect vetting:
  directory: [name]
  found via: [search/competitor/community]
  maintained: [last update visible]
  relevant: [yes/no]
  accepts us: [criteria check]
```

5. Promote monthly. Move vetted prospects into the submission queue; archive the duds with a reason. Expected: a steady feed of new targets.

## Variant phrasings

### discovering new directories over time

Recurring searches, competitor launch watching, and community monitoring, reviewed monthly.

### directory prospecting for devtools

New directories appear constantly; a monthly habit catches them while they are hungry for listings.

### finding directories competitors use

Watch launch-month listings; directories that accepted them will accept you.

## Why it happens

Directories launch, die, and change focus constantly, and search engines lag months behind. The directories most eager for listings are the new ones, which are exactly the ones a one-time search misses. A monthly habit compounds.

## Edge cases / pitfalls

- New directories may have no traffic yet; weigh early-listing advantage against effort.
- Do not submit to a directory the week it launches if it looks half-built; check back in a quarter.
- Keep the prospect list separate from the submission queue until vetted.
- Note who runs the directory; single-maintainer directories vanish without warning.
