Work out how you first subscribed, in the app, in device settings, or on the web. Success check: you can name the billing route before opening any cancel screen.

For an app subscription, open Duolingo, tap the Super icon at the top of the home screen, tap the Settings gear, tap Manage subscription, and select Cancel subscription. Success check: Duolingo hands you to the store step instead of ending on an in app screen alone.

Confirm the cancellation through Google Play, the App Store, or Alipay when prompted. Success check: the store subscription list shows Super Duolingo as canceled with an end date.

For a web subscription, sign in to Duolingo in a browser, click your profile picture, choose Settings, select Super Duolingo, select Cancel subscription, and confirm. Success check: the Super Duolingo settings page shows the subscription will not bill again.

For a subscription started in device settings, follow the route for that device, Apple instructions for iOS or a Duolingo support request for Android as Duolingo directs. Success check: you get a store or support confirmation that automatic billing is off.
