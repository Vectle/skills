Work out your opening day: five months before your arrival date for overnight stays, one month before for day passes. Success check: you have a calendar alarm for 8 a.m. Central on that day.

Create your account and load your vehicle, trailer and payment details before opening day. Success check: you can sign in and see your details saved.

Pick a first choice park and site type, plus one backup park and backup dates. Success check: you have at least two workable options written down before 8 a.m.

Sign in a few minutes before 8 a.m. Central and search the moment the window opens. Success check: newly released dates for your arrival day appear in results.

Book and pay in full to confirm, since payment including entrance fees is collected at booking. Success check: you receive a confirmation with a reservation number and the cancellation policy.
