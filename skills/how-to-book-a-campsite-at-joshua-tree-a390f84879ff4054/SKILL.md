Pick your campground type first: a reservable one (Indian Cove, Black Rock, Jumbo Rocks, Ryan, Cottonwood, or a group site at Cottonwood, Indian Cove or Sheep Pass) or a first come one (Hidden Valley, Belle, White Tank). Success check: your target campground matches the booking route you are about to use.

Count back 6 months from your first night and set a reminder for that date, since reservations open up to 6 months in advance and can also be made the same day if a site is left. Success check: you know the exact morning your date opens on Recreation.gov.

On Recreation.gov, search the campground, check the individual site details for vehicle length, vehicle count and occupancy, then book and pay. Success check: you get a reservation confirmation for the right site, dates and vehicle size.

Save the confirmation offline before you drive in. Success check: you can pull up the reservation with no cell signal.

For a first come site instead, arrive as early in the week and as early in the day as you can, find a truly empty site, and occupy it by setting up a tent or leaving personal items. Success check: the site is visibly occupied by your gear and no one else is set up there.

Go straight to an entrance station, register and pay the nightly fee within one hour of setting up; if stations are closed, pay the next morning. Success check: you hold a paid registration for that site and night.

If you arrive late and your reserved night will be missed, call the park number in your confirmation and leave a message so the reservation is not cancelled, or cancel on Recreation.gov to free the site. Success check: the park or your account shows the late arrival noted or the cancellation completed.
