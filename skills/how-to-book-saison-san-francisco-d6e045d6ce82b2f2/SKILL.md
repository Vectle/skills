# how to book Saison San Francisco

## TL;DR

Saison takes reservations on OpenTable and releases them by the 1st of the month prior, at least a month out, and a credit card is held on file to secure the table. Dinner runs Tuesday through Saturday from 5:00 PM and reservations are required. The cancellation terms get steep inside 48 hours, so book dates you are sure of.

## Steps

1. Work out which release covers your month: tables open by the 1st of the month before it, on OpenTable. Success check: you know the exact 1st to be online for.
2. Book on OpenTable with a card you can leave on file. Success check: you have a confirmation and the card hold is in place.
3. Put the 48 hour line in your calendar the day you book. Success check: you know the exact moment cancellation stops being cheap.
4. Need to change the plan? Contact the restaurant directly by phone or email rather than canceling cold, and do it before the 48 hour window. Success check: a person at the restaurant has confirmed your new plan.
5. If you must cancel early, rebook within 30 days so the early cancellation charge applies as a credit to the new visit. Success check: the credit is attached to a real new date, not forgotten.

## When to use

A set date, a committed party, and a special occasion planned a month or more out.

## When not to use

You are inside 48 hours of a maybe. A late cancel or a no-show is charged the full menu price per person.

## Edge cases

- Cancellation before the 48 hour window is charged 98 dollars per person; inside 48 hours, or a no-show, is charged the full price of the menus selected.
- Credits from an early cancellation can be applied on a rebooking within 30 days, but credits do not apply to no-shows.
- Reservations are required; this is not a walk-in room.

## Community-reported tips (not the restaurant's published policy)

Diners, reviewers and booking trackers report the following. None of it is published by the restaurant, and any of it can change without notice.

1. The dining room stays reservation only, but the separate Bar Experience, offered Tuesday through Thursday, is reported to seat walk-ins when seats allow, though the restaurant strongly suggests booking it. It runs a shorter menu from the same kitchen at a fraction of the dining room price, and regulars treat it as the low-fight way into the room.
2. Saison is easier to book than its reputation suggests. Availability trackers found a table for two open on about half of upcoming dates in their November 2026 readings, so a sold-out first choice is worth retrying on nearby dates before assuming the whole month is gone.
