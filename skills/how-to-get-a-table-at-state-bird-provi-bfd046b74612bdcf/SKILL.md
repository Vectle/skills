Start on the reservations page of the restaurant's own site, which books through OpenTable. Success check: you are in the State Bird Provisions OpenTable calendar for the Fillmore Street dining room.

Match your party to the right route before release day: 1 to 4 guests aim at the dining room or chef's counter, 6 to 8 guests aim at the Alcove Table, and anything larger is a buyout conversation with the restaurant (up to 75 guests). Success check: you know which inventory you are booking when the window opens.

Count back exactly 30 days from the date you want and be on OpenTable at 12:00 PM Pacific when that date releases. Success check: a reminder is set for about 11:45 AM that day, you are signed in, and your party size is already selected.

Book the first time that works for your party and confirm it. Success check: an OpenTable confirmation. A date that has not released yet is not an error; the window rolls forward one day at a time.

Going for the Alcove Table: it is a set menu served family style at a 110 dollar per person food and beverage minimum, with seatings at 6:00 PM and 8:30 PM. Success check: the minimum and the seating time are understood by the whole group before you book.

Missed the release? Use the restaurant's own walk-in route: some seating is kept for walk-in guests on a first come, first served basis, and the restaurant suggests arriving a few minutes before the 5:30 PM opening to be seated with a limited wait, earlier on weekends when demand runs higher. Success check: you are there before opening; there is no published waitlist or line policy beyond that guidance.

## Community-reported tips (not the restaurant's published policy)

The tips below come from diner reports and local guides, not from the restaurant. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- The bar seating is held for walk-ins, and a line forms about 30 minutes before the 5:30 PM opening, earlier on weekends (multiple 2026 reports agree on this).
- One firsthand report: arriving at 4:40 PM put the party second in line and they were seated at open. The walk-in allocation is generally claimed within the first 15 minutes or so of service, so the first seating is the realistic target.
- Tuesday through Thursday are the easiest nights for walk-ins; Friday and Saturday lines form earlier.
- Most openings that surface later are for 9 PM or later seatings (measured tracker data, November 2026), so late seatings are where cancellations land. If prime time is gone, watch for a 9 PM or later table instead.
