Count back 30 days from your target date and set an alarm for a few minutes before 10:00 AM Eastern that day. Success check: you know the exact morning your date opens.

Create or sign in to your Resy account ahead of time and save a payment card, since a deposit is required to book. Success check: your Resy profile is complete and you can book without stopping to type card details.

At 10:00 AM Eastern, open Carbone on Resy, choose lunch or dinner and your party size, and book the first time that works instead of comparing slots. Success check: you receive a Resy confirmation for Carbone at 181 Thompson Street.

If everything is gone, turn on Resy Notify for your date range and a wide time window, and keep phone push alerts on. Success check: Notify is active for at least one date and time range you would actually accept.

When a Notify alert arrives, open it and complete the booking immediately, since the table goes to whoever claims it first. Success check: the alert converts into a confirmed reservation in your Resy account.

Keep checking Resy in the final day or two before your date, when cancellations tend to surface, and consider lunch if dinner stays full. Success check: you have either landed a cancellation or a lunch booking as your fallback.
