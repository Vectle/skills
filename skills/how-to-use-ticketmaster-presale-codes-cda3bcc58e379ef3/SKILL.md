Open the event page on Ticketmaster and read which presales are listed for that date, with their start times. Success check: you can name the presale you qualify for and when it opens.

Get access the right way for that presale. Join the artist fan club on the artist official site, sign up for the venue newsletter, favorite the artist and venue in your Ticketmaster account, sign up for Live Nation All Access, or check your card, Cash App, T Mobile, Verizon, or Spotify account for an offer. Success check: you hold a code, or your account shows you are signed up for a no code Artist Presale before the signup window closes.

For card linked offers, note the exact unlock rule. Cash App uses the first nine digits of an active Cash Card, and Live Nation presales unlock automatically when you sign in with the same email address used for All Access. Success check: you know what to type or which account to sign in with before the sale starts.

A few minutes before the window opens, sign in to Ticketmaster and open the event page. If there is a queue, join it and wait, then enter your code when asked. Success check: you reach the ticket search for the right date.

Tap Unlock next to the Filters button, type the code exactly as it appears, and unlock. Fan club codes are case sensitive. Success check: new seats appear on the map or in the list after unlocking.

Pick your seats fast and check out. If nothing fits, try fewer tickets or singles and pairs, since presale pools are limited and sell out in pieces. Success check: you get an order confirmation, or you know the pool is gone and the general sale is your next shot.

If the code fails, retype it exactly, check you have not passed the ticket limit, and contact the fan club or offer provider, not Ticketmaster, for code problems. Success check: the error is fixed, or the provider confirms the code or window was wrong.
