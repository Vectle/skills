Open the Resy app or resy.com and go to your Reservations, then open the booking you want to move. In the app tap the profile icon and the reservation, then Change or Cancel. On desktop use Manage Reservation or Change Reservation. Success check: you can see the booking details and its cancellation policy on screen.

Read the cancellation policy shown on that booking before you touch anything, and note the cutoff time and any fee. Success check: you know the exact time after which a fee or deposit loss applies, because the restaurant sets it, not Resy.

Tap Change and pick the new date, time, and party size you want. Success check: Resy shows you only slots that are currently available for that party size.

If your new time appears, select it and confirm the change. Success check: the reservation in your account and your confirmation now show the new time, and the old time is gone.

If your new time does not appear, stop and keep the original booking. Search the restaurant fresh for the new slot, and only if you can book that new slot outright should you then cancel the old one, ideally before its fee cutoff. Success check: you hold a confirmed new booking before the old booking is released.

If the app shows no change option at all, contact the restaurant directly and ask if they can move you. Success check: the restaurant confirms the new time in writing or in your Resy account before you rely on it.
