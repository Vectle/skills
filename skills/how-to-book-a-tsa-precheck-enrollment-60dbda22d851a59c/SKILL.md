Start on tsa.gov and open the TSA PreCheck page. Pick CLEAR, IDEMIA or Telos. IDEMIA enrolls through its IdentoGO centers, including some CVS Pharmacy and AAA sites. Telos enrolls through locations including Office Depot and OfficeMax sites. CLEAR lists evening and weekend hours and says no appointment is needed at its locations. Success check: you are on a provider page linked from tsa.gov, not on a lookalike paid helper site.

Submit the initial application online with the provider you picked. TSA says this takes as little as five minutes. Success check: you have a confirmation or pre enrollment record and you know which location you will visit.

Gather the identity and citizenship documents the provider lists for your situation, such as a valid US passport or a driver license plus a birth certificate. Success check: your documents are originals or certified copies, unexpired, and the name matches your application.

Go in person, by appointment or as a walk in where the location allows it. TSA says the visit covers document checks, fingerprints and a photo, plus payment, in about ten minutes. Membership lasts five years and costs 85 dollars or less for a new enrollment, with the exact price set by the provider. Success check: you leave with a receipt and a timeline for your Known Traveler Number.

When you get your Known Traveler Number, add it to your airline profiles and to existing bookings. Success check: the PreCheck indicator shows on a boarding pass for a participating airline.

To renew, go back to the TSA PreCheck renewal page and pick any of the three providers, not only the one you first used. On the TSA page checked in November 2026, IDEMIA listed online renewal at 58.75 dollars and in person at 66.75 dollars, Telos listed online at 69.95 dollars and in person at 58.75 dollars, and CLEAR listed online at 72.95 dollars and in person at 84.95 dollars. Prices change, so confirm on the day. Success check: you get a renewal confirmation and your number stays the same.
