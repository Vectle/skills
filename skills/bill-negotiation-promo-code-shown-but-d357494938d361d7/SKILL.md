Save the proof of the offer before contacting support: a screenshot of the code, the chat or call date, and any confirmation number.

Open the first bill after the offer and check each line item, not just the total.

Check the offer terms for an effective date, a new customer restriction, or a required plan that your account does not have.

Contact billing support in one message with the code, offer date, account email on the offer, and the bill date showing full price. Ask for retroactive application and a courtesy credit for the missed cycle.

Before ending the chat, ask for a case number and written confirmation showing the new amount and the cycles it covers, then save that transcript.
