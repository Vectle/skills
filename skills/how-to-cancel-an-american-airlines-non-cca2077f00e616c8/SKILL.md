Find your trip on aa.com or in the American app and note the fare type shown on the flight details. Success check: you can see whether the ticket is marked non-refundable and which cabin you bought.

If you bought within the last 24 hours and booked at least 7 days before departure, cancel the trip now. Success check: the refund goes to the original form of payment, for any ticket type.

If American moved your flight by 3 or more hours on a domestic trip or 4 or more hours on an international one, decline the new flight and request a refund online instead of accepting credit. Success check: a refund request is filed against the ticket.

Otherwise, cancel on aa.com or in the app before the first flight departs. Success check: the ticket value is preserved as Trip Credit instead of being forfeited.

Submit any refund request while the ticket is still valid, which is 1 year from the ticket issue date. Success check: card refunds are processed back to the original card within 7 business days.

## Community-reported tips (not American Airlines's published policy)

The tips below come from traveler reports, frequent-flyer forums, and consumer guides, not from the airline. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift, and check the current policy before you rely on one.

- On a schedule-changed trip, do not just press Cancel; that path lands you in credit. Screenshot the old and new times first, then claim the cash through aa.com/refunds.
- The refund form works per ticket number. File a separate request for every ticket, and separate requests again for seats, bags, and other extras, or part of your money is never actually requested.
- Know the two thresholds, because agents conflate them: a change of 61 minutes or more buys free-change flexibility, but a refund on a nonrefundable fare needs 181 minutes or more domestic, 241 international (90 minutes counts if the change lands within 72 hours of departure). Hang up and call again when an agent refuses a qualifying case.
- How you cancel decides which credit you get. Cancel on aa.com or in the app and you get Trip Credit: usable for anyone, book-by expiry, 12 months for AAdvantage members (6 months otherwise). Cancel any other way and you get Flight Credit: named traveler only, and travel must begin within 1 year of the original ticket date.
- Before a Trip Credit expires, spend it on a cheaper flight: the leftover comes back as a brand-new Trip Credit with its own fresh 12-month date. The reverse trick, booking a pricier ticket and cancelling, no longer resets the date in recent reports.
- Since July 29, 2026, the 24-hour refund rule only covers bookings made 7 or more days before departure and bought direct with American. Agency and OTA bookings go back to the seller for the refund, and group-block bookings do not qualify.
- The real refund exceptions on nonrefundable fares are narrow and document-driven: death of the passenger or traveling companion, and military orders. Serious illness alone has been refused even with surgeons' notes.
