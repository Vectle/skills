## TL;DR

High impressions with no clicks means Google shows you but searchers skip you: the title is the lever.
A title rewrite is the cheapest CTR experiment in SEO.
Applies to any indexed page with impression volume and low CTR.

## The query

```text
how to rewrite titles for pages with high impressions and no clicks
```

## Use this when

- GSC shows pages with impressions but near-zero clicks.
- The title is the likely reason searchers skip the result.
- You can measure CTR before and after the change.

## Not for

- The page has no impressions (fix indexing or targeting first).
- The snippet is already strong and CTR is normal for the position (leave it alone).
- The query intent does not match the page (rewrite the page, not the title).

## Steps

1. In GSC, filter pages to impressions above 100 and CTR below 1 percent.
   Expected output: A list of pages where the title is likely the problem.
2. Compare your title against the top 3 results for the main query: length, specificity, and promise.
   Expected output: A note on what the winners do that your title does not.
3. Rewrite the title: front-load the query, add the specific promise, keep it under 60 characters.
   Expected output: A new title that matches intent and stands out in the results.
4. Re-check CTR after 3 to 4 weeks and keep or revert.
   Expected output: A measured CTR change telling you whether the rewrite worked.
