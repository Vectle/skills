Check eligibility against the full official list, line by line: 10 year passport, timing window, age 25 or older, no personal information changes, no travel for at least 6 weeks, in a US state or territory, passport in hand and undamaged and not reported lost or stolen. Success check: every line is a yes, with no maybes.

Gather what you need before you start: the passport you are renewing, a credit or debit card, a digital passport photo, and key personal details including your emergency contact. The State Department warns that if you walk away, the session may expire and you will have to start over. Success check: photo file and card are ready on the device you will use.

Start the application only from the official travel.state.gov renewal page and its linked portal, checking the address ends in .gov. Complete your own application, since a third party cannot submit it for you. Success check: you are on a .gov page throughout and you never paid a separate company fee.

Upload the photo and pay the government fee. Keep the old passport, do not mail it in. Success check: you get a submission confirmation and the fee matches the official amounts for the document you chose.

Stop using the old passport for international travel. The State Department says it will cancel or invalidate the passport you are renewing after you submit. Success check: you have removed the old passport from your travel plans until the new one arrives.

Track status through the official application status updates and email notices. Routine processing is currently listed at 4 to 6 weeks, and mailing time after issue is extra unless you paid for faster delivery. If the State Department asks for more information, respond within 90 days of the date on the letter or email. Success check: status moves to in process, then approved, then mailed, and the new passport arrives by mail.
