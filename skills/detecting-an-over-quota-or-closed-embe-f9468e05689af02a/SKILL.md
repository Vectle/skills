## TL;DR

To detect an over-quota or closed embedded typeform, upgrade to @typeform/embed v5.3.0 or later, which added that capability. Before that version the embed gave you no way to tell the form had closed when the plan ran out of monthly quota.

## Steps

1. To detect an over-quota or closed embedded typeform, upgrade to @typeform/embed v5.3.0 or later, which added that capability.

2. Before that version the embed gave you no way to tell the form had closed when the plan ran out of monthly quota.

You are seeing this: Issue Typeform/embed#647 (closed, 11 comments): [FEATURE] A way to know if an embedded typeform is over quota and 'closed'. Use this skill when you run into "Detecting an over-quota or closed embedded typeform: upgrade to v5.3.0".
