## TL;DR

Upgrade to @azure/msal-angular 3.0.4+ with @azure/msal-browser 3.1.0+, which reporters confirmed working. [EmLauber (contributor)]: Angular 17 support is available in @azure/msal-angular 3.x.

## Steps
