Find your next payment date first. Open Manage DashPass in the app or on doordash.com and note the renewal date and whether you are monthly, annual, trial, or on a partner plan. Success check: you can name the exact date you must cancel by.

Cancel at least 24 hours before that payment. DoorDash help says you must cancel 24 hours before the next scheduled payment to avoid the next charge, and its terms say at least one day before the renewal date, giving the example of a January 10 renewal needing cancellation by 11:59:59 pm on January 9. Success check: your cancellation is done a full day or more before renewal.

Complete the flow. Tap the account icon, go to Manage DashPass, tap Cancel Membership, pick a reason, then select Cancel DashPass. Success check: you reach the screen saying the membership has been successfully cancelled.

Save the confirmation. DoorDash says you will receive a confirmation email that the membership has been cancelled. Success check: the email is in your inbox and you have kept it with the date visible.

Check what you keep. On a paid plan, benefits stay valid through the end of the current billing period. If you cancelled during a free period, benefits end immediately. Success check: Manage DashPass shows the end date you expected.

If a trial just converted, act inside 48 hours. DoorDash terms say that if you had a free trial or promotional period, you may cancel within the first 48 hours of your first paid period and receive a full refund of the fee. Success check: you contacted DoorDash or cancelled inside that window and kept the timestamp.

Watch the renewal date. Check your card statement around the old renewal date. Success check: no new DashPass charge posts after the period you already paid for.
