Pay the biometrics fee when you submit your application. The fee is 85 Canadian dollars for an individual applicant and a maximum of 170 Canadian dollars for families applying at the same time, and transit visa applicants pay no biometrics fee. Paying later is allowed but slows the letter down. Success check: you have the payment receipt in or uploaded to your IRCC secure account.

Wait for the biometric instruction letter and watch your account message box, not just your inbox. Online visitor visa, study or work permit applicants who paid at submission usually get it within 24 hours; paying after submission means several days; online permanent residence applicants wait several days to several weeks; mailed applications wait several weeks. Success check: the BIL for this application is in your account and you have noted the date you received it.

Book your appointment immediately through the official collection site for where you are. Outside Canada that is a visa application centre (VAC), in the United States an application support centre or a VAC if you are legally in the US, and inside Canada a designated Service Canada office using its appointment booking tool. Find the site only through the official Canada.ca collection site list. Success check: you have a dated appointment confirmation inside the 30 day window.

If no appointment exists before your BIL deadline, book the earliest one you can and tell IRCC through its web form before the deadline passes, giving the appointment date and uploading a screenshot of the confirmation. Success check: you have submitted the web form and kept the appointment proof.

Attend in person with both required documents: the BIL from your current application and your valid passport. Keep your hands free of henna and let temporary cuts or cracks on your fingers heal first, since poor fingerprints can mean a repeat visit. Success check: the site takes your fingerprints and photo and you leave with the appointment completed.
