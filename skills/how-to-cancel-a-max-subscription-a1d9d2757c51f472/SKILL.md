Find your biller: on the web go to your Subscription page and sign in, or in the app choose your profile then Subscription, or on TV open Settings then Subscription. Success check: you can read who your base subscription is billed through at the top of the screen.

If HBO Max bills you directly, choose Cancel Your Subscription on that page and confirm. Success check: the page shows your expiration date and the cancel option is replaced or a confirmation arrives.

If Apple bills you, cancel on iPhone or iPad in Settings, your name, Subscriptions, HBO Max, Cancel Subscription. Success check: Apple confirms by email and the subscription shows an end date.

If Google Play bills you, cancel in the Play Store under your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, HBO Max. Success check: Google Play confirms and you can still stream until the period ends.

If Amazon, Samsung, or an internet, mobile, or TV provider bills you, cancel in that provider account: Amazon appstore subscriptions, Samsung checkout subscriptions, or your provider site or help center. Success check: that biller confirms the cancellation, not just the HBO Max app.

Note your expiration date and keep streaming until then if you want. Success check: no new charge appears after that date and your Subscription page shows the plan ending.
