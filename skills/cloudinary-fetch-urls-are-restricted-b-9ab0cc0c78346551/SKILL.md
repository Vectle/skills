## TL;DR

Before using fetch delivery, open Console Settings, Security, and either uncheck Fetched URL under Restricted media types or add the source domains to Allowed fetch domains. Whitelisting domains is the safer option since it stops others from burning your quota with their own images.

## Steps

1. Before using fetch delivery, open Console Settings, Security, and either uncheck Fetched URL under Restricted media types or add the source domains to Allowed fetch domains. Whitelisting domains is the safer option since it stops others from burning your quota with their own images. If fetches 401 with a valid URL, check these two settings first.
