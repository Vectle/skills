Check whether your service can be done online first, starting from the DPS appointments page, and only book an office visit if it cannot. Success check: you know whether you need an office visit at all before hunting for a slot.

Open the official DPS scheduler linked from the DPS appointments page and pick the exact service you need, or pick Service Not Listed if yours is not shown. Success check: the scheduler shows offices and dates for that service, or tells you the service can be done online.

Book the earliest workable date at your preferred office, keeping in mind appointments can be scheduled up to six months in advance. Success check: you get confirmation details at the time of booking and can see them again by logging back into the scheduler.

If your office is not listed or has no dates, pick the next closest office and compare its dates rather than waiting. Success check: you have checked at least one other office, since a missing office means it currently has no available appointments.

If you need to go today, keep checking the scheduler through the day for the limited same day appointments DPS makes available at most offices, and grab one as soon as it appears because they fill quickly. Success check: a same day time is booked and confirmed in the scheduler, not just seen on screen.

If you arrive without an appointment, use the self service kiosk in the office to book a same day slot if one is available, or a later day or different location. Success check: the kiosk gives you a booked appointment for today or another day.

Plan to arrive no earlier than 30 minutes before your appointment and no later than 30 minutes after it, since appointments are cancelled after 30 minutes. Success check: you arrive inside that window with the documents your service requires.
