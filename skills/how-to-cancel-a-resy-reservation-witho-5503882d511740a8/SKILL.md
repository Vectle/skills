Find the deadline that applies to your booking: open the reservation in Resy and read the cancellation policy shown there, or check the confirmation email. The terms shown when you booked are the binding version. Success check: you know the exact date and time you must cancel by.

If you are before the deadline, cancel from your reservations list in the Resy app or on resy.com and keep the cancellation confirmation. No fee applies and no charge follows.

If your plans are shaky but not dead, cancel before the deadline anyway and rebook or set a Notify later. Holding a table past the cutoff converts a free exit into a paid one.

If you are already past the deadline, cancel anyway rather than no-showing when the fee is the same either way, and call the restaurant: only the restaurant can waive its own fee, and a polite call before service is the one route that sometimes works.

Never simply fail to show up. Beyond the fee, repeated no-shows can get a Resy account flagged and restricted from making reservations.

## Community-reported tips (not Resy's published policy)

The tips below come from diner reports and reservation guides, not from Resy. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- For scale: published fees at Resy venues commonly run about 15 to 50 dollars per person, and a few rooms charge up to the full price of the meal. The figure on your own booking confirmation is the one that binds, so read it before the deadline, not after.
- If you are already past the cutoff, cancelling still beats no-showing. Venue policy trackers report that some restaurants waive the fee for a late cancellation while almost none waive it for a no-show, and offering to rebook for another date makes a waiver more likely when you call.
