Work out which deadline applies to you, using the vehicle time zone. Standard bookings are free to cancel until 24 hours before the scheduled start. Bookings made within 24 hours of the start are free only for 1 hour after booking. Non-refundable trips are free only in the 24 hours after booking. Success check: you can name your exact free cancellation deadline before you start.

Message the host in Turo messaging as soon as you know, as Turo asks guests to do, but treat that message as notice only. Success check: the host has your cancellation note inside Turo messaging, where there is a record of it.

Open Trips in the Turo app or on the website, open the booked trip, choose the change or manage option, then choose Cancel trip and follow the prompts. Success check: Turo shows you the refund amount for this trip before you confirm, so you can stop if the amount is not what the policy says it should be.

Confirm the cancellation. The cancellation is effective immediately and cannot be undone. Success check: the trip shows as cancelled in Trips and you get a cancellation confirmation from Turo.

Check the refund breakdown against the policy. Inside the free period it should be a full refund. Outside it, trips longer than two days refund the trip price and trip fee minus the average of one day cost, and trips of two days or shorter refund minus half the average of one day cost, with the protection plan, Extras, security deposit, and young driver fee back in full and half of any delivery fee back. Success check: every line of the breakdown matches one of those two formulas or the full refund rule.
