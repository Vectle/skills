Find your confirmation and check whether it says Pay Now or Pay Later, plus your scheduled pickup time. Success check: you know your rate type and pickup time before you start.

Go to hertz.com and open View, Modify and Cancel, then enter your confirmation number and last name. Success check: your exact reservation opens, not a similar one.

Read the cancellation fee shown for your booking before confirming. For US Pay Now bookings expect no fee within 24 hours of booking, 100 dollars more than 24 hours before pickup, and 200 dollars inside 24 hours, capped at the prepaid total. Success check: the fee on screen matches your timing bracket.

Confirm the cancellation and save the cancellation confirmation. Success check: you get a cancelled status and a confirmation you can keep.

For a prepaid refund, wait for the money to return to the card you booked with. Hertz support guidance says refunds generally take about 5 to 7 business days to process, and banks can take up to 10 business days. Success check: the refund minus any fee appears on the original card.

If you cannot cancel online, call Hertz support before pickup time instead of letting it become a no show. Success check: an agent confirms the cancellation and the fee while you are still before pickup.
