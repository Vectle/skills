## TL;DR

phpBB exposes unanswered-post views that most searchers never use.
Answering real unanswered questions is the highest-signal forum contribution you can make.
Works on stock phpBB 3.3 instances; custom themes may rename the links.

## The query

```text
how to find unanswered threads in your niche on phpBB
```

## Use this when

- You do outreach or support on phpBB forums.
- You want high-signal threads where a real answer builds credibility.
- You need a triage method for picking which threads to answer.

## Not for

- The forum is Discourse or NodeBB (use their own search operators instead).
- You want to bump old answered threads (that annoys moderators; answer unanswered ones).
- Your goal is posting links rather than helping (answer first, link later).

## Steps

1. Open the forum index and look for the built-in 'unanswered posts' link near the search bar or use search with the unanswered filter.
   Expected output: A list of threads with zero replies across the forum.
2. Narrow by your niche: search the thread titles for your tool's keywords and sort by start date, newest first.
   Expected output: 5 to 15 recent unanswered threads where your expertise applies.
3. Triage: skip threads older than 60 days, skip ones clearly answered by the asker elsewhere, pick the ones with a clear question.
   Expected output: A shortlist of 3 to 5 threads worth a real answer.
4. Answer the question fully with no links; mention the tool by name only if it is the direct answer.
   Expected output: The reply stands as genuine help and builds the account's credibility.
