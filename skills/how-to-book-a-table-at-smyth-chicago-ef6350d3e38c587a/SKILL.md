Pick your date and count back 90 days. That count gives you your release day. Success check: you have the release date and noon Central written in your calendar.

Set up Tock before release day. Sign in, save your payment details, and decide your party size and which seating times you would accept. Success check: you can reach the Smyth booking page signed in with nothing left to type.

Open the Smyth Tock page a few minutes before 12:00 PM CST on release day. Smyth states reservations are released on a rolling 90 day basis at that time. Success check: the new date appears on the calendar at noon.

Book the first acceptable seating and pay in full. Smyth says reservations from November 5, 2026 onward require full prepayment at the time of booking, replacing the old deposit system, for its single extended Chef's Menu. Success check: you get a Tock confirmation showing the date, time, party size, and the prepaid amount.

Note the dietary limits before you pay. Smyth says it does its best with sufficient notice but cannot safely accommodate the restrictions listed in its FAQ, and asks for questions by writing to the restaurant. Success check: every diner's restrictions are either within what Smyth can handle or already raised with the restaurant.

If your date is sold out, keep checking Tock for returned tables and widen to other nights in the window. Success check: you are checking more than one date rather than waiting on a single night.

If Smyth does not work out, go downstairs to The Loyalist. Smyth's own FAQ describes The Loyalist as its neighborhood restaurant and bar at the same address, with a more casual French bistro style menu and a large bar. Success check: you have a same address fallback for the evening instead of no plan.
