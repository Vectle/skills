Check what canceling will cost before you move. T-Mobile's cancel page says that if you cancel one line and keep others, device payments on the canceled line keep billing monthly, and if the whole account is canceled, remaining device balances become due on the final bill. Promotions tied to a line can also end. Success check: you know the device balance and any credits you will lose.

Back up voicemail, contacts and messages. T-Mobile warns that voicemail is reset after a port and that voicemail and DIGITS history are deleted after cancellation. Success check: anything you need is saved outside the T-Mobile account.

Turn off Port Out Protection on the line if it is active. Only the Primary Account Holder can remove it on postpaid accounts. In T-Life, go to Account, See Plans, Manage add ons, and unselect Port Out Protection for that line, then submit. Success check: the add on no longer shows as active for the line you are moving.

Have the Primary Account Holder create a Temporary Port Out PIN. T-Mobile says to do this on the phone that holds the line, on the T-Mobile network with Wi-Fi off and any private VPN or iCloud Private Relay off. In the T-Life app, go to Account, tap the gear icon, then Permission and controls, then Transfer PIN, then Create PIN, and write the PIN down as soon as it shows. The same path exists on T-Mobile.com from a mobile browser under Profile, Permission and controls, Transfer PIN. Success check: you have the PIN written down and you are ready to use it right away.

Give the new carrier your T-Mobile account number from your bill or account, the Temporary Port Out PIN, and your account PIN or passcode, and ask the new carrier to start the port. Do not ask T-Mobile to cancel the line first. Success check: the new carrier confirms the port request was accepted with those exact details.

Keep the T-Mobile line active until the port completes, then confirm the number works on the new carrier and check your T-Mobile final bill. T-Mobile's cancel page says ported service is billed through the end of the billing cycle, and a final bill follows through your normal billing method. Success check: calls and texts reach the number on the new carrier, and the final bill matches the charges through the cycle end.
