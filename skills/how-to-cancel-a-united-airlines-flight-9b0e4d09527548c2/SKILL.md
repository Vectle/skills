Open My Trips on united.com or in the United app and pull up the reservation with the confirmation number and last name. Success check: the trip displays with a cancel option while it is still before departure.

Check the clock first. If you are within 24 hours of purchase and the flight was booked 7 or more days before departure, cancel the whole reservation for every passenger. Success check: the confirmation says refund to original form of payment, not credit.

If you are past 24 hours, read the fare type on the reservation before confirming. Refundable fares cancel for a refund to the original payment method; nonrefundable fares cancel for a future flight credit. Success check: the cancel screen names the outcome, refund or credit, before you confirm.

Confirm the cancellation and save the confirmation. Success check: you receive a cancellation confirmation and the refund or credit shows in your account or email.

If United canceled or significantly changed the flight and you do not want the alternative offered, request a refund instead of accepting a credit, through My Trips or the United refund form. Success check: a refund request is submitted and acknowledged.

## Community-reported tips (not United Airlines's published policy)

The tips below come from traveler reports, frequent-flyer forums, and consumer guides, not from the airline. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift, and check the current policy before you rely on one.

- If United changes your schedule and you want cash, do not click accept on the new itinerary in Manage Reservations. Request the refund instead. Travelers report refunds granted at around a 2 hour change in practice; the guaranteed federal line is 3 hours domestic and 6 hours international.
- When the online free one-time change tries to charge a fare difference after a United cancellation or schedule change, stop and call. Agents repeatedly fix it for free, per September 2026 FlyerTalk reports.
- Hang up and call again is a real tactic here, but arrive with a specific alternative flight and a concrete reason (the new layover is too short, you will arrive too late). United commonly allows rebooking within about 250 miles of the original airport.
- A future flight credit's year runs from the original ticket purchase, not from when you cancelled, and it is a travel-by date, not a book-by date. Cancelling again does not reset it.
- To travel after a credit's travel-by date, travelers report booking a trip that starts before the deadline and then using Change Flight to move it later. The book-and-cancel version of this trick has been reported hit and miss since 2024.
- If a credit has already expired, frontline agents will say no. In one documented 2024 case, escalation through a consumer advocate got an expired $3,162 credit converted into an Electronic Travel Certificate (a book-by instrument) as a goodwill gesture. Rare, and not a right.
