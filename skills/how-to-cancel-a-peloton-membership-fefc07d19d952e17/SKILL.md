Go to the Peloton membership page and sign in, then look under Memberships to confirm whether you have All Access, an App membership, or both. Success check: you can name the exact membership you are cancelling.

For All Access or an App membership billed by Peloton, click Manage next to that membership, then Pause or cancel, and choose cancel. Success check: the flow shows a cancellation choice for that membership.

If you only need a break, choose pause for 1, 2 or 3 months instead at that same screen and note that the pause starts at the next billing cycle and billing resumes automatically. Success check: the screen shows your chosen pause length.

For an App membership bought through a third party, cancel in that store instead, for example Settings then your name then Subscriptions then Peloton on Apple, or Payments and Subscriptions then Subscriptions in Google Play on Android. Success check: that store shows the Peloton subscription as cancelled.

Finish every prompt and keep the confirmation. Success check: you have a confirmation showing access runs to the end of the current billing period and will not renew.

A few days before your old billing date, check that no new charge is scheduled, and if the page will not complete the cancellation call Member Support at 866 679 9129. Success check: no renewal charge appears after the period ends.
