Book direct on the venue own website using its Reserve button, which is where its SevenRooms book lives. Success check: the booking flow shows the venue branding and your confirmation comes from the venue.

Create a complete guest profile with the same name, phone, and contact details every time, and add honest notes about occasions, seating, and dietary needs. Success check: your details match across bookings so the venue history builds in one profile.

Dine there, show up on time, and keep the profile active with repeat visits. SevenRooms says venues can prioritize frequent or recent diners, loyalty members, and high spenders. Success check: you are a repeat guest the venue can recognize in its own records.

When a date looks full, join the waitlist or submit a request if the venue offers one, and watch for a Priority Alert by text or email. Success check: you get an alert for an opening and can claim it quickly before it goes to the wider waitlist.

If the venue offers you Private Line or another direct messaging channel, use it for last minute requests and special needs instead of calling repeatedly. Success check: the venue replies in that channel and can book or change a table from the conversation.

If you are referred by a regular, let that guest use any referral link the venue sent them. SevenRooms says venues can reward referrals from loyal guests. Success check: your booking is linked to the referral the venue issued.
