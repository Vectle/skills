## TL;DR

Keyframe images must come from a CDN URL you host yourself; pass a random hotlinked URL and the job dies on frame moderation.

## Steps

1. For Luma video keyframes, frame0 with an image URL starts from a still, a generation id extends forward, and interpolate needs both frames as completed generations. Host keyframe images on your own CDN and verify completion before referencing prior generations.
