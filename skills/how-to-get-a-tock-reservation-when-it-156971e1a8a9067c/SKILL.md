Open the business page on Tock and search your date, party size and time to confirm it is really sold out and to note any next release date and time shown on that page. Success check: you can see the sold out state and any release line for that venue.

Tap Notify next to the sold out experience and submit a request with the experience, party size, one or more dates, a time and optional notes. Success check: the request appears with your reservations in the Tock app or browser as an active waitlist request.

Turn on email and, if you use the Tock app, push notifications, since Auto Notify alerts matching guests by email and push. Success check: notifications are enabled so an alert will reach you.

Add separate requests for other dates, times or party sizes you would truly accept, deleting and re adding a request if your details change. Success check: each acceptable option has its own accurate request.

When an alert arrives, open Tock immediately and complete the full booking, including any prepayment the experience requires. Success check: you have a confirmed reservation in your profile, not just an alert.

Keep checking the venue page close to the date as well, since released and cancelled seats are venue specific and can reappear near the date. Success check: you have checked both your Notify alerts and the live page before giving up.
