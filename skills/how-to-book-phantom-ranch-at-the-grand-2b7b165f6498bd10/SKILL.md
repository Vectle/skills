Pick your stay month and count back 15 months. For example, entries for a stay month are taken from the 1st to the 25th of the 15th month before it, so a November entry window is for a stay 15 months later. Success check: you have one target month and its exact entry dates written down, with no dates spilling into the next month.

Before the window opens, decide your party size, nights, and whether you want a cabin or dorm bed, plus meals and duffel service to add if you win. Non-group hiker bookings are limited to one reservation per person, a maximum of 4 nights, and a maximum of 9 people. Success check: your request fits those limits and everyone in the group can travel on those dates.

Enter online during the 1st to 25th window. Go to the Phantom Ranch page on Grand Canyon Lodges and choose Enter the Phantom Ranch Lottery. Entries cannot be made by phone and there is no fee to enter. Success check: you receive an email acknowledgement with a recap of your submission. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

Wait for processing in the following month. Xanterra says submissions are processed, winners notified, and reservations guaranteed by the end of that month, and winners get up to 10 days to pay to confirm and guarantee the booking and add meals or duffel service. Success check: you either hold a guaranteed reservation after paying inside the 10 day window, or you have a clear not selected result and can move to the next step.

If you do not win, target the general sale. Unsold and unclaimed inventory, and all Phantom Ranch meals, are released to general availability under the 13 month reservation window, bookable online or by phone. Success check: you can search current Phantom Ranch availability for your month in the separate Phantom Ranch booking engine.

Keep checking availability after general sale, and protect a booking you land. Phantom Ranch has a 45 day cancellation policy: cancel at least 45 days before arrival for a full deposit refund, and forfeit the deposit inside 45 days. Success check: any booking you hold shows the correct names and dates, since lottery submissions are non-transferable and must stay under the original name and email address used to enter.
