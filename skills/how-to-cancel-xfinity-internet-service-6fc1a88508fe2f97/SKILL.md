Gather your account details, the service address, your chosen disconnect date, and a list of every rented device in the home, such as the gateway, TV boxes, remotes, and power cords. Success check: you can name each device Xfinity will expect back.

Check whether you are on a term agreement. If you are, ask what early termination fee applies if you cancel before the term ends, because the fee comes from your agreement and newer no contract plans may have none. Success check: you know the fee amount, or that no fee applies, before you confirm.

Start the cancellation by calling 1-800-934-6489 and saying cancel service, by scheduling a callback from the Xfinity cancel page, or by going to an Xfinity Store. Success check: you are talking to an agent who can process a disconnect, not general support.

Decline the retention offers you do not want and state your disconnect date clearly. Agents will usually offer a discount, a cheaper plan, or a pause first. Success check: the agent confirms the exact date service and billing stop.

Before you hang up or leave, get a confirmation or reference number and ask whether a confirmation email will follow. Success check: you have written proof of the request with the date and time.

Return all rented equipment within 10 days of disconnection. Drop it at an Xfinity Store for a receipt on the spot, start a return in the Digital Return Center for a prepaid UPS label, or drop it at a UPS Store. Only the device, its remote, and its power cord need to go back. Success check: you hold a store receipt or UPS tracking for every device.

Watch for the final bill and check it line by line for charges past the disconnect date, any early termination fee, and unreturned equipment charges. Success check: the final bill matches the disconnect date and shows no charge for equipment you returned.
