## TL;DR

If your new forum account gets flagged as spam on the first post, stop posting immediately. Read the moderator notice, send one polite appeal explaining the situation, then wait several days. If the account is restored, rebuild with link-free replies only. Never argue, and never create a second account to get around it.

```text
forum account flagged as spam on first post how to recover
```

## Use this when

- A new account's first post was auto-flagged or held as spam
- You received a moderator warning about spam-like behavior
- The account is restricted but not yet banned

## Not for this skill when

- The account is permanently banned (see the ban-appeal skill instead)
- You were actually spamming (own it, apologize, move on)
- You are tempted to make a replacement account (dont)

## Steps

1. Stop all activity on the account. No more posts, no edits to the flagged post, no new threads. Expected: nothing you do in the next 48 hours can make it worse.

2. Read the notice carefully. Distinguish an automated spam filter hold from a human moderator warning; the wording usually tells you. Expected: you know whether a bot or a person flagged you.

```
flag triage:
  automated hold:  "pending review", "flagged by the system"
  human warning:  named moderator, specific rule cited
```

3. Send one polite appeal. Explain briefly that you are new, what you were trying to do, and ask what to fix. No links in the appeal, no demands, no essay. Expected: message sent, tone respectful.

4. Wait at least 3 to 5 days. Moderators are volunteers; nudging the appeal looks like the spam behavior that got you flagged. Expected: a reply, or silence you can interpret after a week.

5. If restored, rebuild link-free. Go back to the warming sequence: helpful replies, no links, for at least two weeks. Expected: the account accumulates clean history.

6. Log the incident in your venue notes with the cause you suspect. Expected: the next account on a similar venue avoids the same trigger.

## Variant phrasings

### new forum account marked as spam what to do

Stop posting, appeal once politely, wait, then rebuild with link-free activity if restored.

### forum spam false positive recovery

Automated filters misfire on new accounts with links. One calm appeal and patience beats any workaround.

### first post held for spam review

Do not repost it. Wait out the review, and if it clears, treat the account as brand new for warming purposes.

## Why it happens

Spam filters score new accounts aggressively because almost all forum spam comes from fresh accounts. A first post with a link, a URL-like string, or even certain phrasing patterns can trip the filter with no human involved. It is usually not personal and often not even reviewed by a person unless you appeal.

## Edge cases / pitfalls

- Appealing twice or arguing resets the clock and annoys the exact people who can help.
- Editing the flagged post to remove the link sometimes clears automated holds; check if the venue allows it.
- If the appeal is denied, accept it and abandon the venue rather than creating a new account there.
- Note the trigger (link in first post, too many links, phrasing) so your other accounts avoid it.
