Pick your target month and set a reminder for the first day of the month before it, at 3:00 PM Eastern. Success check: for a January visit, your reminder fires on December 1 at 3:00 PM.

Create a Tock account and save your payment card in advance, since every reservation is paid in full at booking. Success check: you can complete a Tock checkout without entering card details under time pressure.

Decide in advance which experience you want, the Chef's Counter with seatings at 5:30 PM and 8:45 PM, or the Bar Tasting Menu at 6:00 PM and 9:15 PM, and rank several dates and both seatings. Success check: you have a written fallback list of dates and seatings you will accept.

Sign in a few minutes before 3:00 PM, open the Atomix page on Tock, and refresh as the month opens. Success check: the full following month shows as bookable, not just a single new date.

Book your best available date and seating and complete the prepaid checkout right away. Success check: your card is charged and the reservation appears under Upcoming Reservations in your Tock account.

If your dates sell out, join the Tock waitlist for those dates and keep an eye out for released seats. Success check: you are on the waitlist for at least one date you can actually attend.
