Pick your zone before you apply: Core Enchantment, Colchuck Lake, Snow Lake, Stuart Lake, or Eightmile/Caroline. A Core permit lets you camp in any zone; the others limit you to that zone. Core is by far the most competitive, and Snow or Colchuck still allow day hikes into the basin.

Apply on Recreation.gov between February 15 and March 1: choose your entry date, zone, and group size, and pay the 6 dollar application fee. Success check: the application shows in your Recreation.gov account.

Check results on March 17 in your Recreation.gov account. If you won, confirm the permit and pay the per-person fees by March 31, or the permit goes back into the pool.

If you lost, watch Recreation.gov after the confirmation deadline: unconfirmed and leftover permits are released first-come, first-served, and cancellations return to inventory through the season.

In season, use the daily lottery as the last route: it runs on a phone from inside a geofence near the trailheads, you apply the day before your start date, and it covers a small share including 8 Core spaces a day. You must physically be near Leavenworth, so it only works once you are already there.

## Community-reported tips (not the agency's published rules)

The tips below come from trip reports, hiking forums, and permit guides, not from the agency. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift.

- Zone choice is the biggest lever in the draw. An analysis of the Forest Service's published 2024 results put success at about 2 percent for applications listing the Core, 3 percent for Colchuck, 7 percent for Snow, 9 percent for Stuart, and 14 percent for Eightmile/Caroline.
- Unclaimed permits come back first come, first served on April 1 at about 7 am Pacific, right after the March 31 confirmation deadline, and they go fast; be logged in and ready before the hour, not at it.
- Applying on the first day of the window does not improve your odds. Every application filed between February 15 and March 1 is drawn equally, so file carefully rather than early.
- If you are aiming at the Core, enter the smallest group you would actually accept; smaller groups fit the spaces the draw has left and report better success.
