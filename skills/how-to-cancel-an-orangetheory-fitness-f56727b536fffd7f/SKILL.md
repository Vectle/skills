Find your home studio and your billing date. Your home studio is the studio on your Membership Agreement, and studios are independently owned and operated. Success check: you know the studio name, your next billing date, and the date 30 days from today.

Decide cancel versus freeze. If the break is temporary, ask the studio about a freeze of 30 to 60 days, up to 2 times per year. If you want out for good, skip the freeze and go to cancellation. Success check: you have picked one path and can say why in one sentence.

Submit the cancellation request in writing to your home studio, or start it on the official Orangetheory cancellation information page and follow up with the studio. Keep the request clear: full name, home studio, and that you are giving 30 days notice to cancel. Success check: you have a submitted form, a sent written request, or a signed form copy with the date on it.

Ask the studio to confirm the effective date and the last payment. Any payment scheduled within 30 days after the studio receives your request will be charged as scheduled, and you keep access for 30 days from your last payment to use unused sessions. Success check: the studio gives you a final billing date and an end date in writing.

Use or lose your remaining sessions before the end date. Remaining sessions and promotional rates are forfeited after that window. Success check: your calendar shows your last planned class before the end date, or you accept that the sessions will lapse.

Check your card statement after the final billing date. Success check: one final scheduled payment at most inside the notice window, and no new dues after the confirmed end date. If a charge appears after that date, contact the home studio first with your written confirmation.
