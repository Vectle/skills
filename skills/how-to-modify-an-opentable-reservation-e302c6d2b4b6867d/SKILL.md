Check the restaurant cancellation and no-show policy first, in your confirmation email or on the restaurant OpenTable profile. Some restaurants allow same day changes, others want 24 to 48 hours of notice, and a late change can still trigger a fee. Success check: you know the deadline and any fee that applies to your booking before you touch it.

Open the booking. On opentable.com, sign in, open your profile or the calendar icon, and find the booking under Upcoming reservations. In the app, open Reservations on iOS or History on Android. In the confirmation email, use the Modify button in the email itself. Success check: you are looking at the correct restaurant, date, and party size for the booking you want to change.

Select Modify, then change the time, date, or party size to the new details you want. Success check: the screen shows your new time, date, and party size together before you confirm, with no warning that the slot is unavailable.

Confirm the change and review the confirmation page. Success check: the updated details appear on the confirmation page and a fresh confirmation email arrives showing the new time, date, and party size, which means the original booking was updated rather than duplicated.

If there is no Modify option, or the booking does not appear in your account, do not keep retrying. You may have booked while signed out or under a different email address, or the restaurant may use a stricter policy. Contact the restaurant directly and ask them to move the booking. Success check: the restaurant confirms the new details back to you, ideally in writing or in an updated confirmation.
