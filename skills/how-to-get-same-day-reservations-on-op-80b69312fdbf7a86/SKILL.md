Sign in to OpenTable on the web or in the app and search your restaurant, or your area, for today with your real party size. Success check: you see live times for today, or a clear no availability result for the times you asked for.

Widen the search before giving up: try earlier and later times around your target and, if the group allows it, a smaller party size. Success check: you have checked times on both sides of prime time, not just your first choice.

If nothing matches, select Notify me, or Priority notify if that label shows for your account, and set the party size, todays date, and the widest start and end time window you would truly take. Success check: the alert shows as set, and in the app it appears under Reservations and Alerts.

Turn on push notifications for the OpenTable app, and keep email or SMS alerts on if that flow offered them. Success check: OpenTable is allowed to send you notifications on your phone.

Keep refreshing the search yourself through the day, especially when plans commonly change, instead of waiting only for the alert. Success check: you are checking live availability, not a cached result from earlier.

The moment a matching time appears, in an alert or in your own search, complete the booking right away and review any cancellation or no show policy shown before you confirm. Success check: the reservation appears under Upcoming Reservations and you get a confirmation, since the alert alone reserved nothing.

If your plans change after booking, cancel through your confirmation, the site, or the app as early as you can. Success check: the reservation shows as cancelled, not as a no show, which protects your account standing.
