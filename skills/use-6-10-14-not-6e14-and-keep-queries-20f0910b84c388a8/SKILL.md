Write Wolfram queries like a search box, not a sentence: short English keywords, and scientific notation as 6*10^14 rather than 6e14 - the engine parses the caret form and chokes on the e-notation. Long natural-language questions get misinterpreted; strip them to the computation you actually want.

Context: LlamaIndex Wolfram Alpha tool reference: the tool works best with scientific notation like 6*10^14 rather than 6e14, and natural-language questions should be simplified to short English keyword queries.
