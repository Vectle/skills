Install the Resy app, sign in, complete your profile, and save a payment card, since many in demand restaurants require a card to book. Success check: your profile shows the card on file and you stay signed in.

Search each target restaurant in the app and add it to your favorites so all of them sit in one list. Success check: every target appears in your favorites list when you open it.

Open your favorites list with your date and party size set and scan the live availability shown for each place. Success check: you can see bookable times, or a clear no availability state, for each favorite without searching again.

On any favorite with no tables for your date, tap the Notify button with the bell icon and set your party size, preferred date and preferred time range. Success check: the Notify shows as set for that restaurant and date in the app.

Turn on push notifications for Resy in your phone settings and in the app, since Notify alerts also go by email but push is faster. Success check: Resy is allowed to send push notifications on your device.

Repeat Notify for each backup date and wider time window you would genuinely take at that restaurant. Success check: each date you would accept has its own Notify set.

When an alert lands, tap it immediately and complete the booking inside Resy. Success check: you receive an email confirmation, since the alert alone does not hold the table.
