Open the confirmation and identify the rate name and its cancellation deadline and penalty. Success check: you know whether you hold Best Flexible, Book Early and Save, or a Reward Night, and the exact cutoff.

For a direct booking, open Manage Your Stay on ihg.com or in the app, review the deadline and penalty shown, then cancel and save the cancellation confirmation. You can also contact IHG Customer Care or the hotel. Success check: you hold a cancellation confirmation for the right dates and room.

For a Best Flexible booking cancelled in time, expect no charge, and if you prepaid any amount, expect the refund process to run to your original payment. IHG notes cash refunds can take up to two billing cycles to appear on a statement. Success check: no fee is charged and you know the slow refund window before chasing it.

For a Book Early and Save booking, do not cancel expecting money back. The rate terms say full prepayment, nonrefundable, no refunds if cancelled or changed. Success check: you have made an informed choice to keep, use, or forfeit the booking.

For a Reward Night cancelled within the hotel policy, check your account right away. IHG says points return instantly. Success check: your IHG One Rewards balance shows the points back the same day.

For Points and Cash, remember the cash part bought points at booking and is nonrefundable. On a valid cancellation you get points back, including the purchased points, not cash. Success check: the full points amount, not a cash refund, is back in your account.

Keep the cancellation confirmation until points or money have landed. Success check: you can quote it to Customer Care if a redeposit is delayed, noting IHG warns day of arrival cancellations can face review and delay.
