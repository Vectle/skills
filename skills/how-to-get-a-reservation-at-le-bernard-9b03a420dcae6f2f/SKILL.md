Work out your release day. For any date next month, your day is the first of this month. Success check: you have the first of the month and 7:00 AM Eastern in your calendar.

Before the day, set up Resy with your account logged in and a credit card saved, since a card is required to secure every reservation. Success check: you can book on Resy without stopping to add a card.

At 7:00 AM Eastern on the first, book on Resy right away and take a workable time if your first choice is gone. Prime evening slots go first. Success check: Resy shows a confirmed reservation for your date and party size.

If online fails, call the restaurant at 9:30 AM Eastern the same morning when phone reservations open. Success check: the reservations team confirms availability or waitlist options by phone.

Protect the booking once you have it. Cancel at least 48 hours ahead if plans change, or you face a 150 dollar per person fee. Success check: any change you make is more than 48 hours before your seating.

If you get nothing, use the lounge plan. Arrive early in service and ask for lounge seating on a walk in basis. Success check: you are seated in the lounge without a reservation, or you know the wait before you commit.
