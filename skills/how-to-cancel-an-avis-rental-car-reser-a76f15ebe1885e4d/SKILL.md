Work out which booking type you have. Check your confirmation for Pay Now, which means you paid the rental period and extras at booking, or Pay Later or Pay at Location, which means you did not prepay. Success check: you can say which type you have and whether any money was taken up front.

Check the clock against your scheduled pickup time. For a United States Pay Now booking, cancelling at least 24 hours ahead keeps the fee at the lower tier. Success check: you know whether you are outside or inside the 24 hour window before you submit the cancel.

Go to the Avis View, Modify or Cancel flow on avis.com or in the Avis app, or call reservations, and enter the country, the last name on the reservation and the confirmation number. Success check: the correct reservation opens with the right location, dates and car class.

Choose cancel, read the fee and refund amount shown for that specific booking before you confirm, and only then submit. Terms can vary by country and by the exact offer, so the screen for your booking wins over any general guide. Success check: the confirmation screen shows a cancellation number and the refund amount or fee that will apply.

Save the cancellation confirmation and watch the original payment card. For Pay Now, the refund is the prepaid amount minus the cancellation fee, and guides citing the Avis United States terms put the timing at about seven business days in many cases. Success check: you have the cancellation record saved and the partial refund posts, minus 50 dollars outside 24 hours or minus 150 dollars inside 24 hours for a standard United States Pay Now booking.

If you booked Pay Later, confirm the cancel completed even though no refund is due. Success check: you get a cancellation confirmation and no charge follows, in the usual case where no credit card guarantee fee applies.
