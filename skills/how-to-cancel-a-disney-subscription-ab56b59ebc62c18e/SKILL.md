Sign in to Disney+ in a browser and open your profile, then Account, and read the Subscription section to see who bills you. Success check: you can name the billing party shown on the page, such as Disney, Apple, Google Play, Amazon or a provider.

If Disney bills you directly, select your subscription and choose Cancel Subscription, then work through the prompts and any optional survey until the final confirmation. Success check: the subscription page shows it will end at the end of the current billing period.

If Apple bills you, open Settings on an iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, pick Disney+ and tap Cancel Subscription. Success check: Disney+ shows an end date in your Apple subscriptions list.

If Google Play bills you, open Google Play, tap your profile icon, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, pick Disney+ and cancel there. Success check: Google Play lists the subscription as cancelled with an expiry date.

If Amazon or a phone or TV provider bills you, cancel from that account's memberships or plan perks area instead of the Disney+ site. Success check: you get a confirmation from that biller, not just from Disney+.

If you signed up on a Roku device, manage or cancel the subscription through Disney+ as Roku directs Disney+ subscribers back to Disney+. Success check: your Disney+ account page, not the Roku menu, shows the pending end date.

Note the end date and keep watching until it arrives if you want. Success check: no new charge appears after that date, and the plan shows as expired or offers to restart.
