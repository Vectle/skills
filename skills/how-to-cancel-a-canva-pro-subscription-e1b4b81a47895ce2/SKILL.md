Check your billing email or card statement to see who charged you: Canva, Apple, Google Play or another provider. Success check: you know which biller holds the plan before you look for a cancel button.

If Canva billed you, sign in on the web to the exact account and team that holds the subscription, then open your profile, Settings, then Billing. Success check: your paid plan is listed on the Billing page.

Select Cancel plan on the plan, then choose Continue cancellation through the pause offer, the features you will lose screen, and the reason prompt. Success check: you reach a final Cancel subscription confirmation, not just the pause screen.

If Apple billed you, cancel in device Settings under your name, Subscriptions, Canva. If Google Play billed you, cancel in Play Store, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Canva. Success check: the store shows the plan as cancelled with an end date.

For a team plan, confirm you are signed in as an owner or administrator and in the right team before cancelling, and warn members first. Success check: the Billing page shows the team plan, and members know paid features end at the period end date.

Save the cancellation confirmation email and note the end date. Success check: the plan shows as cancelled and you still have paid features until that end date.
