Read the cancellation line on your confirmation email and note the deadline in the hotel local time and the fee for missing it. Success check: you know the exact cutoff and penalty before you touch anything.

Sign in at hyatt.com, open My Reservations, open the stay, and choose Cancel, then Confirm. You can also cancel in the World of Hyatt app or by phone. Success check: you receive a cancellation email with a cancellation number.

For a flexible cash rate cancelled in time, expect any deposit to be refunded to your original payment method. Success check: no cancellation fee appears and the deposit refund is pending or posted.

For a points booking cancelled in time, wait for the points to return, which Hyatt terms put at about 3 days. Success check: your World of Hyatt balance is restored within about 3 days.

For Points plus Cash cancelled in time, check both halves: points back to your account and the cash part refunded. Success check: both the points balance and the card refund show up.

If you are an Explorist, Globalist or Lifetime Globalist member and the hotel deadline is 48 hours, remember Hyatt gives those tiers cancellation up to 24 hours before arrival on many rates. Success check: your reservation shows the shorter deadline applying to your booking.

Keep the cancellation email until the refund or points are fully back. Success check: you can follow up with the hotel with the cancellation number in hand.
