Open your booking in the Resy app or on resy.com and read the exact cancellation policy, cancellation time, and amount paid. Success check: you can name the deadline and whether your payment was a prepayment, a deposit, or a card hold.

If you are still before the cancellation time and you no longer want the table, cancel the booking online from that same booking screen. Success check: the booking shows as cancelled and you get a cancellation confirmation.

Wait for the automatic refund to your original payment method. Resy prepaid guidance puts this at about 3 to 5 business days. Success check: the refund appears on the original card or payment method, not as a Resy credit.

If you cancelled after the cancellation time, or a fee was already charged, contact the restaurant directly, explain briefly what happened, and ask them to refund or to toggle a refund during cancellation in their system. Success check: the restaurant confirms a refund amount and you later see it on the original payment method.

If the restaurant agrees but nothing arrives, follow up with the restaurant first with your booking date, name, and amount, before disputing anything with your bank. Success check: you have the venue confirmation of the refund to show your bank if you still need it.
