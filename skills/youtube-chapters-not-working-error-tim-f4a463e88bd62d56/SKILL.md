Check that the first timestamp in the list starts the video at its beginning.

Check that there are at least three timestamps, in increasing time order, with no duplicates.

Put each timestamp and its chapter title on its own line in the description, with nothing else mixed into those lines.

Remove any extra characters before the first timestamp and any links pasted inside the chapter lines.

Save the description and reload the watch page in a fresh tab.

If chapters still do not appear, check in YouTube Studio whether automatic chapters are overriding or the video is still processing.
