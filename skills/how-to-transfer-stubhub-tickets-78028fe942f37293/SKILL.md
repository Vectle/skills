Open your StubHub order and read the delivery type before touching anything: barcode, mobile transfer, AXS, PDF, or another delivery method named on the order. Success check: you can say which type you hold and therefore which account the transfer happens in.

For barcode tickets, stay in StubHub. Open the order, select Manage Transfers, enter the recipient email address twice, choose the number of tickets, confirm, and select Send. If Manage Transfers is not there, these tickets cannot be transferred this way. Success check: StubHub shows the transfer as sent to the exact email you entered.

For mobile transfer or AXS tickets, leave StubHub and sign in to the original vendor account where the tickets live, such as Ticketmaster or AXS. Open the order there, select Transfer, choose the seats that match your StubHub order, and enter the recipient information from the StubHub order details. Success check: the vendor account shows the transfer completed to the right person, and you have kept a screenshot of the completed transfer details as proof.

For PDF or other file delivery on the order, follow the delivery instructions on the order itself and send the recipient the actual delivered file rather than a picture of it. Success check: the recipient can open the file and see the full ticket for the correct event, date, and seats.

Make the recipient accept before event day. For barcode tickets they use the Accept ticket button in the StubHub email and sign in or create a StubHub account to see the tickets in their own Orders. For vendor transfers they accept in the vendor account, ideally signed in with the same email the transfer was sent to. Success check: the tickets appear in the recipient account, not just in an email inbox.

If the recipient cannot find or accept the tickets, fix the address rather than resending blindly. A transfer sent to a misspelled email can be recalled or cancelled in the original vendor account and resent to the corrected address, and a barcode transfer can be cancelled from Manage Transfers any time before it is accepted. Success check: the new transfer shows as accepted in the recipient account.
