Open Manage Trips on jetblue.com, pull up the booking, and start the cancellation before the scheduled departure time. Success check: the booking shows a cancel option and has not yet departed.

If you booked 7 or more days before departure and you are within 24 hours of booking, cancel the entire booking. Success check: no cancellation fee is charged for the cancellation.

On a nonrefundable fare, complete the cancellation and accept the outcome shown: any per person fee for your fare option comes off, and the remaining balance is issued as a JetBlue travel credit. Success check: the credit shows in your JetBlue travel credit account.

On a Flex fare, cancel before departure and choose the refund. Success check: the refund is headed to your original form of payment, typically 7 to 10 business days for a credit card.

Write down the credit expiration date. Success check: you are planning against 12 months from the original ticketing date, and you know that rebooking with the credit does not extend it.

## Community-reported tips (not JetBlue's published policy)

The tips below come from traveler reports, frequent-flyer forums, and consumer guides, not from the airline. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift, and check the current policy before you rely on one.

- The Travel Bank trap: credit expires 12 months from the original ticketing date, not from when you cancelled. Cancel in month 10 and you hold about 2 months of credit, not a fresh year. It is a book-by date, though; the trip itself can be later. Booking with the credit and cancelling does not restart the clock.
- Cancel or change online in Manage Trips. Phone and chat both add a $25 per person service fee on top of any fare fee (Mosaic members are exempt from the service fee). There are no credible recent reports of chat or hang-up-call-again beating JetBlue's published fees; the honest community move is self-serve.
- A schedule change is refund leverage: if JetBlue moves you 3 hours or more (domestic) or 6 hours or more (international), adds a connection, or changes your airport, you can rebook within 5 days either side for free, or cancel for a refund to your original payment method, even on a nonrefundable fare.
- Mosaic status does not waive the Blue Basic cancellation fee. The only Mosaic carve-out is the same-day switch fee.
- Blue Basic cannot be changed at all, only cancelled, minus $100 (North America, Central America, Caribbean) or $200 (all other routes), with the rest going to Travel Bank credit. On a cheap Basic fare the fee can eat the whole value, so price it before you cancel. Blue and above cancel fee-free to credit.
- Travel Bank credit cannot be transferred between accounts, but there is no name lock: stay signed into your own account and spend your credit booking a ticket for someone else. Only one credit account can be used per booking.
- Do not let credit expire on an agent's promise of an extension. In one November 2026 firsthand case, a traveler who followed that advice got back $487 of $1,461, good for only 14 days. Spend or book before the original-ticketing anniversary instead.
