Sign in on the Services and subscriptions page with the exact account that bought the plan. Success check: the Microsoft 365 plan is listed under that account.

Find the plan and choose Manage. If you see Turn on recurring billing instead, stop here. Success check: that wording means the plan is already set to expire and will not be charged again.

Decide which exit you want before you click. Choose Cancel to trigger the refund eligibility check, or turn off recurring billing to run out the paid term with no refund. Success check: the next screen matches the exit you picked.

Follow the Cancel prompts to the end and read the refund outcome on screen. Success check: the page confirms cancellation and states whether a refund applies and when access ends.

Save the confirmation and note the end date and the storage change date. Success check: you have the end date written down before you close the tab.
