Count back six months from your arrival date to find your booking morning. Success check: you have a calendar reminder for 8 AM Pacific on that exact date.

Create your ReserveCalifornia account and save your details and payment method days or weeks ahead. Success check: you can sign in and reach checkout details without typing anything new.

Pick your park and campground in advance and note a couple of backup sites or parks. Success check: you have a first choice and at least one backup ready before release morning.

On release morning, sign in 10 to 15 minutes before 8 AM Pacific and load your park, dates and party details. Success check: at 8 AM you are one search away from seeing the new date.

At 8 AM Pacific, search and add your site to the cart right away, then check out without pausing. Success check: you get a confirmed reservation, and you know the non-refundable reservation fee of 8.25 dollars was charged.

If your date is sold out, keep checking back for cancellations, especially in the final weeks before arrival. Success check: you either book a reopened site or settle on a backup park.
