Open your confirmation and check two things: whether you prepaid, and your exact pickup time. Success check: you know if the prepaid fees apply to you and how many hours you have left.

Go to the Enterprise view, modify or cancel flow and pull up the reservation. Success check: your booking opens with the correct location and dates.

If you did not prepay, cancel and confirm. Success check: you get a cancellation confirmation with no fee.

If you prepaid, check the clock before confirming. More than 24 hours before pickup the US fee is 50 dollars, inside 24 hours it is 100 dollars, and the rest of your prepayment is refunded. Success check: the fee shown matches the 24 hour bracket you are in.

Confirm the cancellation and save the confirmation. Success check: the reservation shows as cancelled and you have the record if a refund query comes up.

Watch for the refund to your original payment method. Enterprise refund guidance is payment method dependent, with debit refunds typically taking about 5 to 10 business days and cash or check refunds sent by mailed check within 20 business days, so use those ranges as your wait window and contact the branch if nothing arrives. Success check: the refund minus the stated fee posts, or you have a branch follow up open.
