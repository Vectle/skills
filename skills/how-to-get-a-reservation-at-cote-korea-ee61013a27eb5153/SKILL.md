Confirm you are looking at the New York Flatiron listing, not Miami. Success check: the address reads 16 West 22nd Street, New York, and the listing is dinner only.

Count back 30 days from the date you want and set a reminder for just before 10 a.m. ET that day. Success check: you know the exact release morning for your date.

Sign in to Resy before 10 a.m., set your party size, and book as soon as the new date opens at 10 a.m. ET. Success check: you get a Resy confirmation for the date and time you picked.

If Resy shows nothing, call the restaurant right away. The official COTE site lists phone reservationists daily from 12 p.m. to 10 p.m., and the Resy guide from COTE operations says part of the room is held for phone bookings. Success check: a reservationist tells you whether any phone held tables remain.

If both are full, turn on Resy Notify for your date and party size. Success check: Notify is set and you get an alert if a cancellation opens a table.

Read the cancellation terms on your confirmation before you finish. Success check: you know the late cancel and no show fee that applies to your booking, COTE charges 50 dollars per person for cancellations inside 24 hours on its published terms.
