Open a web browser and go to your Spotify account page and sign in. Success check: you see your current plan name and your next billing date.

Check the Payment section under Manage your plan to confirm who bills you. Success check: you can name the biller, Spotify directly or a partner company.

If Spotify bills you, select Cancel plan under Your plan at the top of the screen, or under Manage your plan, or select Cancel subscription if that label shows. Success check: Spotify starts the cancel flow instead of a plan change screen.

Continue through the screens to the confirmation message. Success check: you reach a confirmation that the plan will switch to free.

Note the date shown. Success check: Premium still works now, and the account is set to switch to free on the next billing date.

If there is no option to change the plan, follow the partner contact link shown under Payment on the account page and cancel with that partner. Success check: the partner confirms the subscription will end and no further charge is scheduled.
