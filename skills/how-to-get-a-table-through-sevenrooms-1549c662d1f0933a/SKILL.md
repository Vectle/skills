Go to the restaurant official website first and tap Reserve or Book a Table. Success check: a booking widget opens in the venue branding, often on a SevenRooms address, for the correct location.

Enter date, party size, and time and review every seating area or experience shown. Success check: you see live times for that venue only, not a list of other restaurants.

Before confirming, read the upgrade and add on offers on screen, such as a premium table, welcome drink, cake, or a chef experience, and add only what you want. Success check: your total and inclusions match what you selected, with any deposit or minimum spend clearly shown.

Enter your contact details and any card the venue requires for its policy, then confirm. Success check: you receive a confirmation from the restaurant with a link to view or modify the booking.

If your time is sold out, look in the same flow for a waitlist or Priority Alert option if the venue offers one. Success check: you are on the alert or waitlist for that venue and date, or you have a note to recheck the venue page directly.
