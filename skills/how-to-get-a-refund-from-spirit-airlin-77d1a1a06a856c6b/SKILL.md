Cancel in My Trips on spirit.com before the cutoff: changes and cancellations close 1 hour before scheduled departure. Success check: the cancellation is confirmed while the flight is still more than an hour out.

If you are within 24 hours of booking and the flight was booked 7 or more days before departure, take the refund. Success check: the confirmation shows a refund to the original form of payment, the only routine cash refund Spirit offers.

On a Value fare outside that window, expect the tiered cancellation fee to come off your value: no fee at 60 or more days before departure, $59 at 31 to 59 days, $79 at 7 to 30 days, and $99 inside the last week. Success check: you know the fee before you confirm, because if the fee exceeds the ticket value the credit is zero.

On Spirit First and Premium Economy bookings there is no cancellation fee, but the value still lands as a Reservation Credit unless a refund exception applies. Success check: the full reservation value shows as credit.

Save the Reservation Credit details and its expiration date somewhere you will find them. Success check: you know the credit amount and the date it dies, because an expired Spirit credit is not reissued.

## Community-reported tips (not Spirit Airlines's published policy)

The tips below come from traveler reports, frequent-flyer forums, and consumer guides, not from the airline. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift, and check the current policy before you rely on one.

- Status note: multiple May 2026 reports say Spirit ceased operations on May 2, 2026. These tips describe how cancellations and credits worked during its operating period, which is what matters for tickets and credits from that era.
- Date your advice before you trust it. The fee ladder in this playbook applies to the basic Go/Value fare under the rules reinstated in February 2025; bundled fares (Go Savvy, Go Comfy, Go Big) stayed fee-free, and posts saying Spirit has no cancellation fees at all are from the May 2024 to February 2025 window.
- Reservation credits issued after May 2024 last 1 year from issue, but they are single-use and named-passenger only, and any leftover value is lost on the first use. Spend the credit on a booking that uses as much of it as possible. The 60-day and 90-day figures in older posts are the pre-May 2024 rule.
- If Spirit changed your flight by 60 minutes or more, or delayed it by more than 2 hours, do not accept the rebooking if you want your money back. Declining it preserves a cash refund to your original payment; traveling on the new flight ends that eligibility.
- A credit offered instead of an owed refund is a different instrument from a voluntary credit. In one April 2026 firsthand case it was valid for 5 years, with a cash refund issued by default if the passenger made no choice by departure. Do not confuse it with the 1-year voluntary credit, and do not accept the short credit when a refund was owed.
- Channel order for a straightforward cancel: My Trips first, then chat, text (48763), or WhatsApp, with the phone saved for split or complex bookings. Travelers document human channels taking hours, so start early, and screenshot the cancellation confirmation and credit code yourself when you cancel. Firsthand reports show confirmation emails and credits going missing, with disputes settled only from the passenger's own screenshot.
