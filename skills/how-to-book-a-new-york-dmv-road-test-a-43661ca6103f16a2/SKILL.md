Gather what DMV says you need to schedule: a valid New York learner permit, an original unexpired Pre Licensing Course Certificate MV 278 or Student Certificate of Completion MV 285, the ZIP code of where you want to test, and at least 1 unused road test. Success check: you have all four in hand before opening the scheduler.

Open the Schedule a Test link from the DMV Schedule and Take a Road Test page and search by ZIP code. Success check: the system offers the earliest available dates and times at sites near that ZIP code.

Pick the earliest date and site that work, remembering tests are almost always on weekdays and only rarely on a Saturday. Success check: you have a booked appointment at a named test site, not just a date you saw listed.

If your area is too far out, search a different ZIP code in another county or area, since DMV says you do not need to test where you live. Success check: you have compared at least one other area before settling for a long wait.

Alternatively, schedule, reschedule, or cancel by phone on the DMV road test number listed on the official page. Success check: the phone booking is confirmed and matches the date and site you wanted.

Before test day, check the DMV cancellations, closings, and delays page, since DMV may cancel, reschedule, or relocate tests for weather or road construction. Success check: your test is still on at the same site on the morning you travel.

Arrive up to 15 minutes before your test with your physical photo learner permit, corrective lenses if your permit requires them, your original certificate, and a properly registered, insured, and inspected vehicle with a licensed accompanying driver. Success check: the examiner can verify your identity, documents, and vehicle and start the test.
