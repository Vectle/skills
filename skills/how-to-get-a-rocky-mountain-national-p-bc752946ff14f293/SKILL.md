Decide your permit type from your trailhead. Anything on Bear Lake Road means Timed Entry plus Bear Lake Road, everything else means standard Timed Entry. Success check: your permit type matches your first destination of the day.

Note your release date. Permits release May 1 for May 22 to June 30, then June 1 for July, July 1 for August, August 1 for September, and September 1 for October dates, all at 8 AM Mountain time. Success check: you know the exact first of the month release for your trip month.

Create your Recreation.gov account in advance and be logged in before the release. Success check: you are signed in and ready before 8 AM Mountain time.

Book your date and your 2 hour entry window and pay the 2 dollar non refundable Recreation.gov processing fee. A park pass or entrance fee is separate. Success check: you get a confirmation for the correct date, permit type and window.

If the monthly release sells out, try again at 7 PM Mountain time the night before your arrival date, when additional permits release first come first served. Success check: you are on Recreation.gov right at 7 PM, since these go fast.

Arrive inside your window. For Bear Lake Road you must enter the corridor inside your 2 hour window, and re entry to that corridor is only after 2 PM. Success check: you enter in your window and keep your confirmation reachable offline.
