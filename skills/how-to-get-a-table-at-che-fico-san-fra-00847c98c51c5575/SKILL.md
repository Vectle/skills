Try the reservation link on the Che Fico site first for your date and party size. Success check: a confirmed table, or a clear no to work from.

No table? Plan the walk-in: arrive in person and put your name on the nightly waitlist for the dining room or the bar. Success check: your name is on the list; they cannot add you over the phone, so do not try that route.

If the wait is long, stay close: guests are welcome to have a drink in the bar area while they wait. Success check: you are still on site when your name comes up.

For groups over six, skip the public flow and send a large group or private dining inquiry instead; over six counts as a large group here. Success check: the events side is quoting your party, not the booking widget.
