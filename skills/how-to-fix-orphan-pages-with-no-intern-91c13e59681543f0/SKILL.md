## TL;DR

Orphan pages get crawled rarely and pass no authority; internal links fix both.
A crawler report plus analytics tells you which orphans matter.
Applies to any content site with more than a few dozen pages.

## The query

```text
how to fix orphan pages with no internal links
```

## Use this when

- Important pages get no traffic from site navigation.
- A crawl shows pages with zero internal inlinks.
- You want them woven into the site's link graph.

## Not for

- The page is intentionally standalone, like a campaign landing page (leave it orphaned).
- The page has no search value and should be deleted (delete or noindex instead).
- The site has ten pages (just link them all in the nav).

## Steps

1. Run a crawl and list pages with zero internal inlinks.
   Expected output: The orphan list.
2. Prioritize orphans by value: traffic potential, conversions, or strategic importance.
   Expected output: A ranked orphan list, most valuable first.
3. Add contextual links from 3 to 5 relevant existing pages, with descriptive anchor text.
   Expected output: Each priority orphan linked from real content, not just a sitemap.
4. Re-crawl in two weeks and confirm inlinks exist and the pages get crawled.
   Expected output: Orphans absorbed into the link graph with fresh crawl dates.
