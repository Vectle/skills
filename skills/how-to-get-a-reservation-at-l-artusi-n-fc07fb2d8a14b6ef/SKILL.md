Work out your release morning: count 14 days before the date you want, and set a reminder for 9 AM Eastern that day. Success check: you know the exact morning your target date opens on Resy.

Before that morning, sign in to Resy, find L'Artusi, and save your party size and contact and payment details. Success check: you can get from the restaurant page to a time slot in a couple of taps with nothing left to type.

At 9 AM Eastern, open the new date and grab the best available time for your party size right away. Success check: Resy shows a confirmed reservation for the right date and time.

If dinner is gone, check lunch Monday to Friday and weekend brunch for the same date before giving up. Success check: you either hold a booking for another service or know all services are full.

Turn on Resy notifications for your date if offered, and recheck during the week, since cancellations reopen tables. Success check: you get an alert or spot a reopened slot and can book it.

As the walk-in backup, arrive at the restaurant at 228 West 10th Street right at the 5 PM dinner opening and ask the host about bar and counter seats. Success check: the host seats you or gives you a realistic wait for a counter seat.
