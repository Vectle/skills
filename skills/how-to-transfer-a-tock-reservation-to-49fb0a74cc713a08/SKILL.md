Sign in to Tock and open the reservation confirmation in your profile, or open the confirmation email and click Manage. Success check: you are looking at the correct date, time, and party size for the booking you want to move.

Look for the transfer option on that reservation and select it. Success check: Tock opens a transfer flow for that specific reservation rather than a general account page.

Enter the recipient details Tock asks for and set the amount as a gift or as a price at or below what you originally paid. Success check: the amount shown is not above the original purchase price.

Send the transfer and tell the recipient to watch for the Tock invitation and complete it promptly. Success check: the recipient can open the invitation from Tock and start accepting the transfer.

Confirm the finished state in Tock before the visit, and do not start a second transfer while one is already in progress. Success check: the reservation now sits with the new owner in Tock, not just in an email thread between you.
