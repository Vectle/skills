Open My Trips on delta.com and pull up the ticket. Look for the word Nonrefundable next to the cabin name. Success check: you know your fare type, Basic, Classic, or refundable Extra, before you cancel.

If you are within one day of purchase on a qualifying US originating eTicket, cancel now. Success check: the confirmation shows a full refund with no cancellation fee, including any prepaid fees.

If you are past the window and the fare is refundable, cancel in My Trips for a refund to the original form of payment. Success check: a refund confirmation is issued.

If the fare is Basic or Classic nonrefundable, cancel before departure anyway. Success check: the remaining value lands in your account as an eCredit, minus the cancellation charge for your fare and route, with its expiration date shown.

Do not let the flight depart uncanceled. Success check: the cancellation is confirmed before the scheduled departure time, because Delta gives uncanceled tickets no remaining value.

## Community-reported tips (not Delta Air Lines's published policy)

The tips below come from traveler reports, frequent-flyer forums, and consumer guides, not from the airline. Treat them as street knowledge that can drift, and check the current policy before you rely on one.

- A Delta schedule change is leverage for changes Delta normally would not allow: agents will often drop a leg, change airports (policy allows origin or destination within 100 miles), or shift dates by a couple of days to keep your money rather than refund it. If the first agent says no, hang up and call again.
- Research the exact flight numbers and times you want before you call or chat. Agents say yes more easily to a specific request than to an open-ended one.
- In Delta messaging, open with the word Representative to skip the automated assistant. Outcomes are then as agent-dependent as the phone. Note that Delta's old iMessage and text support shut down in October 2026; the surviving route is messaging inside the Delta app.
- After a Delta cancellation, do not tap the big CREDIT button in the app if you want cash. Look for the small Refund, request refund instead path, and explicitly request a refund to your original payment. One traveler documented a $428 refund approved this way in December 2024 after the app defaulted to credit.
- Voluntary-cancel eCredits expire 1 year from the original purchase date, not from when you cancelled. Only a credit you took instead of a refund after Delta cancelled or significantly changed your flight gets 5 years.
- Most eCredit extension tricks no longer work. The one still reported working in September 2026 buys about 30 days at a time: book a trip for the last day or two before expiry, check the new ticket's expiration date immediately to confirm it moved, repeat as needed, and cancel for eCredit 72 hours after the final repeat.
- Basic fare cancellation-fee waivers do happen as one-off courtesy via messaging. In one August 2025 case the $199 fee was waived and the traveler paid only a $29.99 fare difference. A courtesy, never an entitlement.
