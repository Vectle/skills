Work out your release date first: six months to the day before arrival, at 8am Pacific. Success check: you have the release date and time in your calendar with a reminder the day before.

Create a ReserveCalifornia account in advance and save your vehicle and camper details. Success check: you can sign in cleanly before release morning with no password reset needed.

Shortlist several acceptable sites across camps before release, mixing premium riverfront and standard sites. Success check: you have at least five site numbers ranked, so one sold out site does not end the attempt.

Sign in before 8am Pacific on release day, search Pfeiffer Big Sur for your dates, and book the best available site on your list. Success check: you reach checkout for a real site in your date range.

Pay and save the confirmation, noting the non refundable reservation fee and your check in time of 2pm. Success check: you have a confirmation with site, dates, and party details that match your trip.

If release morning fails, keep checking ReserveCalifornia for cancellations and remember the cutoff: no online or phone bookings inside 48 hours of arrival. Success check: any later find is still outside that 48 hour window or you plan to ask in person at the kiosk.
