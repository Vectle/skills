Confirm you are on the Maryland system, the park reservation site or the call center number on the official park page, not Recreation.gov. Success check: the listing says Assateague State Park and shows standard sites at the state park rates and electric sites at the higher rate.

Set your booking morning 365 days before arrival and be ready before 9 a.m. Eastern. Success check: you are signed in with payment and vehicle details ready before inventory appears.

Search your exact weekend at 9 a.m. and filter for electric only if you truly need it, otherwise search all site types to widen your chances. Success check: you see live sites for your arrival date instead of a not yet open message.

Book the first workable site and complete payment. Success check: you get a confirmation naming Assateague State Park, your loop or site, and your dates.

If the weekend is sold out, try the adjacent Thursday or Sunday night pattern, a non electric site, or a shoulder season weekend and set a reminder to recheck for cancellations. Success check: you hold either a confirmed backup or a dated recheck plan.
